# Roadmap to the first beta (Lotus, 2026-10-06)

The order Lotus set. Each stage finishes before the next starts.

1. **Now: finish everything that isn't art.** The Builder chain keeps going: mechanics, systems, maps, balance, the Fun Gate, bugs, menus. The goal is a game that is **fun with no art at all**. Story text waits: Lotus is rewriting the story from the Fable pass's draft 7 (PR #1144, open for his review), so don't write new scenes, supports or cards on the old text while he works. Engine and content hooks the story will need are fair game.
2. **Lotus finishes the story.** He edits draft 7 into his version, and #1144 merges with his changes.
3. **The partners work his story in:** the scenes, cards, supports and endings, through the house writing process, with every change visible to him.
4. **The no-art beta.** Lotus plays the whole campaign start to finish. **It has to be fun without the art first.** Whatever isn't gets fixed.
5. **Art.** Lotus secures the art (his own Blender work on the Quaternius bases, the Nichol portraits after his recast, commissions as he can), and Code integrates it.
6. **The first beta for other people.**
