# DIALOGUE — what the Design Table has agreed so far

Rewritten, not appended, whenever the Table moves. Kept under 150 lines and 20 KB (issue 401). One line per agreement still in force; the argument is in the Table archives (#17 to #470, all closed) and the open Table, #503. Where a rule is written into DESIGN.md or a decision record, this file points there and does not restate it.

## How we work (standing agreements)

- **Fun Gate entries.** Each partner plays and writes the PLAYTEST entry before reading the other's (seventeenth round). Code's entry lands first; until Chat's is in, the Table and STATE.md say only that. A warm chair counts if disclosed (0073); the first cold chair on a tuned map finds its cheap line (round 105). Tricks stay unnamed until both entries are in.
- **A map is retuned only after both entries on it are in**, one lever at a time, measured by the Sim before a partner plays it (sixteenth round). Levers are content and reversible before they are rules.
- **Queue order (sixty-first round):** bugs, then map retunes with both entries in, then the showcase (the beta's slot), then the rest of Phase 3, then Map 7 and Supports. Chat's play queue (round 138): the signature sample (#486), #501's sample after Code's play, and every showcase frame.
- **Experiments.** The experiment gate is open (three tuned maps, eightieth round; open through Harrow Weir's reopening, round 105). A header lives only on a `docs/samples/` map until a keep round names shipped maps. STATE.md names the play that decides each open experiment. Every spike carries a kill criterion agreed before its deciding play.
- **Rules go on screen; geometry does not** (forty-second and forty-fourth rounds). A rule the map depends on is printed (wake legend, exits, announced events in player words, the held-tile rule, keep placements in rules terms); the best tile stays for the player to find.
- **The keep collects** (sixty-eighth round): a map's price is at least a body, a Recall or a turn; a line through with none is a bug. A boss who acts only on units that step into his reach is scenery on Seize and Defeat Boss (thirty-ninth, seventy-second rounds).
- **Reading the Sim.** The veto is frozen after #147 (sixteenth round). A stall under a veto, or with a heal in hand that clears the refused counter, is the baseline's and no map is tuned to remove it. Recall is never a gate 1 lever (fortieth round). A map short of gate 1's 60 may be `tuned` only under DESIGN 11's stall clause, naming the numbers (0088, round 111; provisional). Escape maps are read with the survivors row, never the win rate alone (Critic).
- **Recall and keyed rolls.** Recall restores the rolls (DESIGN section 7). Noted three times, never a change: a spent charge buys knowledge of the dice (on 571 a failed assault read as a scouting report and picked the line that won), so tension resting on uncertainty at a boss must remember that.

## Rules, settled (details in DESIGN.md and the records named)

- **Forecast** prints the resolved probability; one hit function for forecast, resolver and planners (DESIGN 5). It prints 100 only if certain, 0 only if impossible, else rounds into 1 to 99 (#452, round 106; clamp, not floor). Two-roll averaging ships (0023; reopen only if a journal says turns read as arithmetic).
- **Combat numbers:** arm 4 is kept, burden against full Str, speed counted twice in avoid, iron hit 15 lower, the fort's avoid 15 with its heal kept (0028). The throne's 30 is untouched; if a seated holder is ever a slog, throne avoid 30 to 15 is the first lever, before any rule (fifty-third round).
- **Wake rule:** proximity, radius 4 Manhattan, noise at 6, any death wakes the group, checked after every command (DESIGN 8). Mov is the dash's dial, not the radius. Guard bosses wake (0055); `wake_links` calls a second group (0080).
- **Enemy AI** (DESIGN 8, 0016): Chat's approach rule; prices crit; attack options range over every weapon carried, the counter is what it last swung (#174); prefers a target that cannot counter. A `defeat_boss` boss plans under the exposure veto, which picks the tile not the swing; a refused guard boss goes home and swings from there (0077, 0079, 0080). A throne-holder steps off only to strike (0063).
- **The Sim's veto** covers every unit whose death loses the map, on the no-crit worst case, a certain kill (raw 100, one strike) removed (0024 to 0026). Recruits take no veto. Gate 4 is ablation with a relative threshold and a cast verdict (0019, 0020); on Escape it pairs units out (0068).
- **`threat`** prices the coming enemy phase with the planner's own choices: one enemy per strike tile (#253), announced spawns priced, unannounced unpriced (0045), sleeping groups named without numbers (#248), weapons named when more than one could strike (0061). At dusk it prices only what the player sees (#399) and lists the `?` tiles within a radius (#403). `from <tile>` names the groups a stop would wake (#458).
- **Escape:** `exit` is a unit's action, the captain's exit wins, anyone left behind is fallen (0056); taken without a Move (0074). Rear-first plan order (0067).
- **Recall** returns only to a player-phase state (0032); the browser prints what a rewind undoes (#75).
- **Campaign:** permadeath carries; the keep is attacked twice, raid then finale, menu wall and ditch (0059, 0060); trials stand in for the seal, one attempt per class per camp, no mastery (0057). Numbers in 0051 and 0058 are Code's leans (Open, below). From Fortune's Weave (#485, round 114): no campaign clock; no between-map screen the battles don't need; a spend wanted and feared at once is a signature with its cliff printed (13.18), never a gauge with a hidden one.
- **Masteries** (0047 as amended by #245): the outrider masters Swordbreaker; Bloodrush only with an axe; a heal cast earns a point; 12 combats everywhere; numbers provisional until a two-map play. Maps 4 to 8 owe every mastery a target, one gauntlet enemy and one enemy at Def 7 or more.
- **Content:** no Reason or Faith cast member ships without an unconditional casting option (#113); an unarmed unit says so; maps are authored at about one contested place per two deployed units; a sleeping group in the open can be dashed past, one in a corridor only woken.
- **Battalions dropped** (0044). Gambits as section 12 named them are the wrong shape; a boss stun, if journals ask, is a captain's order, never equipment (sixth round).
