# DIALOGUE — what the Design Table has agreed so far

Rewritten, not appended, whenever the Table moves. Kept under 150 lines and 20 KB (issue 401). One line per agreement still in force; the argument is in the Table archives (#17, #134, #216, #265, #322, #364, #420, all closed) and the open Table, #470. Where a rule is written into DESIGN.md or a decision record, this file points there and does not restate it.

## How we work (standing agreements)

- **Fun Gate entries.** Each partner plays and writes the PLAYTEST entry before reading the other's (seventeenth round). Code's entry lands first; until Chat's is in, the Table, STATE.md and the PR say only that. A warm chair counts if disclosed (0073); the first cold chair on a tuned map is the play that finds its cheap line (round 105). Tricks stay unnamed until both entries are in (fourteenth round).
- **A map is retuned only after both entries on it are in**, one lever at a time, measured by the Sim before a partner plays it (sixteenth round). Levers are content and reversible before they are rules.
- **Queue order (sixty-first round):** bugs, then map retunes with both entries in, then the Godot client and its readability pass (the beta), then the rest of Phase 3, then Map 7 and Supports. Chat's play queue (round 119): #131, then both #481 samples, then a signature sample, then the Tollgate pincer sample (#495).
- **Experiments.** The experiment gate is open (three tuned maps, eightieth round; open through Harrow Weir's reopening, round 105). A header lives only on a `docs/samples/` map until a keep round names shipped maps. STATE.md names the one play that decides each open experiment. Every spike carries a kill criterion agreed before the deciding play.
- **Rules go on screen; geometry does not** (forty-second and forty-fourth rounds). A rule the map depends on is printed (wake legend, exits, announced events in player words, the held-tile rule, keep placements in rules terms); a map's best tile stays for the player to find.
- **The keep collects** (sixty-eighth round): a map's price is at least one of a body, a Recall or a turn; a line through with none is a bug. A boss who acts only on units that step into his reach is scenery on Seize and Defeat Boss (thirty-ninth, seventy-second rounds).
- **Reading the Sim.** The veto is frozen after #147 (sixteenth round). A stall under a veto, or with a heal in hand that clears the refused counter, is the baseline's and no map is tuned to remove it. Recall is never a gate 1 lever (fortieth round). A map short of gate 1's 60 may be marked `tuned` on a Table decision naming the numbers only under DESIGN 11's stall clause (0088, round 111; provisional; Harrow Weir: short 4, at least 25 proven stalls). Escape maps are read with the survivors row, never the win rate alone (Critic).
- **Recall and keyed rolls.** Recall restores the rolls (DESIGN section 7). Noted twice, never a change: a spent charge buys knowledge of the dice, so tension resting on uncertainty at a boss has to remember that Recall removes it.

## Rules, settled (details in DESIGN.md and the records named)

- **Forecast** prints the resolved probability; one hit function for forecast, resolver and planners (DESIGN 5). It prints 100 only if certain, 0 only if impossible, else rounds into 1 to 99 (#452, round 106; clamp, not floor). Two-roll averaging ships (0023; reopen only if a journal says turns read as arithmetic, one roll the first dial).
- **Combat numbers:** arm 4 is kept, burden against full Str, speed counted twice in avoid, iron hit 15 lower, the fort's avoid 15 with its heal kept (0028). The throne's 30 is untouched; if a seated holder is ever a slog, throne avoid 30 to 15 terrain-wide is the first lever, before any rule (fifty-third round).
- **Wake rule:** proximity, radius 4 Manhattan, noise at 6, any death wakes the group, checked after every command (DESIGN 8). Mov is the dash's dial, not the radius (third round). Guard bosses wake (0055); `wake_links` calls a second group (0080).
- **Enemy AI** (DESIGN 8, 0016): Chat's approach rule; prices crit; attack options range over every weapon carried, the counter is what it last swung (#174); prefers a target that cannot counter (twenty-ninth round). A `defeat_boss` boss plans under the exposure veto, the veto picks the tile not the swing, and a refused guard boss goes home and swings from there (0077, 0079, 0080). A throne-holder steps off only to strike (0063).
- **The Sim's veto** covers every unit whose death loses the map, on the no-crit worst case, a certain kill (raw 100, one strike) removed (0024 to 0026). Recruits take no veto. Gate 4 is ablation with a relative threshold and a cast verdict (0019, 0020); on Escape it pairs units out (0068).
- **`threat`** prices the coming enemy phase with the planner's own choices: one enemy per strike tile (#253), announced spawns priced, unannounced unpriced (0045), sleeping groups named with the wake rule and no numbers (#248), weapons named when more than one could strike (0061). At dusk it prices only what the player sees (#399) and lists the `?` tiles within a fixed radius (#403). `from <tile>` names the groups the stop would wake, unpriced (#458).
- **Escape:** `exit` is a unit's action, the captain's exit wins, anyone left behind counts as fallen (0056); taken without a Move (0074). Rear-first plan order in the Sim (0067).
- **Recall** returns only to a player-phase state (0032); the browser prints what a rewind undoes (#75).
- **Campaign:** permadeath carries; the keep is attacked twice, raid then finale, menu wall and ditch (0059, 0060); trials stand in for the seal, one attempt per class per camp, no mastery (0057). Numbers in 0051 and 0058 are Code's leans and still the Table's (Open, below). From the Fortune's Weave reading (#485, round 114): no campaign clock, since our pressure lives inside maps; the between-map layer never gains a screen the battles don't need; a spend wanted and feared at once is only ever a recruit's signature with its cliff printed (13.18), never a gauge with a hidden cliff.
- **Masteries** (0047 as amended by #245): the outrider masters Swordbreaker; Bloodrush only while wielding an axe; a heal cast earns a point; 12 combats everywhere; numbers provisional until a two-map play. Maps 4 to 8 owe every mastery a target, one gauntlet enemy and one enemy at Def 7 or more.
- **Content:** no Reason or Faith cast member ships without an unconditional casting option (#113); an unarmed unit says so; maps are authored at about one contested place per two deployed units (seventh round); a sleeping group on open ground can be dashed past, one in a corridor only woken (third round).
- **Battalions dropped** (0044). Gambits as section 12 named them are the wrong shape; a boss stun, if journals ask for one, is a captain's order, never an equipment layer (sixth round).

- **13.13 The pincer: kept on its sample, the anvil arm too** (0082, 0083; the rule is DESIGN 13.13; Code seed 97, Chat cold seed 113). A pin costs the anvil's action; the planner sets pins (#419); `threat` prints anvil plans as unpriced `anvil:` lines (#457). Deciding play in (round 119): Chat's cold play of `brackwater_cut_pincer.map` (seed 499; Code seed 443) landed zero enemy pins in seven phases, two printed plans denied, one at a cost, and one player-made pin that killed; the kill clause did not fire. Read with it: a cork in a corridor cannot be pinned, so the Sim's anvil counts on that sample measure the heuristic's formation, not the arm's bite; the squeeze (a Mov 4 cork must leave by turn 6) is the Escape rule's arithmetic. Held: a pinned unit cannot counter; on a seated slog, pin the holder first, else throne avoid 30 to 15. Keep round (#495): `the_tollgate_pincer.map`, the Sim counting player pins beside enemy pins; the enemy arm cannot fire there (no Aggressive group-mate), so it reads the player's arm; killed if in both plays no unit is moved to make or deny a pin.
- **13.14 Brace: passed its kill criterion** (0084; Code seed 307, Chat cold-to-rule seed 439, clause 1 met on turn 9; the rule is DESIGN 13.14). On `brace: on`, a unit that Waits on the tile it began its phase on is struck at -15 hit until its side's next phase; the pin's slot, so a pin and a brace cancel. A sleeping Guard never braces. A vetoed boss keeps his brace; "a vetoed boss never braces" is the first lever if a boss has no bait window. Keep round: `saltmarsh_ford_brace.map` (#430), after Chat's #131 re-rate, read on the turn the leader dies and his turns with no forecast above 50; if it shows no deliberate bait of a hold, holds stop bracing and the arm stays for guards and bosses. #157 moves up when 13.14 is kept.
- **Pin and brace are tuned in displayed numbers**, as is any rule in that slot: brace acts mid-band where TwoRollAverage widens it, the pin near the top where it flattens, so at the same raw 15 brace is the stronger (ninetieth, ninety-first rounds).
- **13.15 Wildfire (#435, built #437, 0085; the rule is DESIGN 13.15):** behind `wildfire: on`, a Cinder hit on a unit on forest lights the tile; fire burns 20 percent of max HP at its side's phase start and at player phase start goes to plain and lights orthogonal forest. The planner prices fire and the next front. `the_tollgate_wildfire.map` tests the player's arm; the keep round wants an aggressive hexer. Killed if in both plays no strike is taken for an ignition over a better forecast and no unit refuses or leaves a tile for fire or the front; the strike clause alone keeps only "Cinder scorches forest". Code seed 457: refuse clause met once, warm. Keep signal: the front's bill for an earlier ignition seen coming and paid on purpose.
- **13.16 The windup (#441, built #443, 0086, accepted; the rule is DESIGN 13.16):** behind `windup: on`, a `windup` weapon (the Post Maul, range 1) raises a blow over the target's tile, no roll, no counter; at its side's next phase start it lands on whoever stands there, certain, a normal hit's damage. Only a hit the wielder could counter breaks it; `threat` and the forecast print the landing (#444). Sample `the_tollgate_windup.map` (the warden swapped for a Toll Mauler). Keep signal: a door-holder striking the maul under 100 knowing a miss lands it, or a sturdier unit swapped in to take it. Killed if in both plays nobody gives up a tile, strike or turn to leave a blow and no strike on the wielder is taken to break one over a better forecast, or if either journal calls the dodge free; a free siege owed only to the swap is resampled once on a guarded tile. Keep round: the maul over the tile the player most needs.
- **13.17 Overwatch (#481, built #490, 0089; rounds 112 to 116; the rule is DESIGN 13.17):** behind `overwatch: on`, `watch <unit>` (equipped weapon reaching 2) shoots the first foe to end a Move or Canto exactly two tiles away, once, before it acts, no counter; any strike on the watcher ends it; exclusive with brace; `watch` prints the strike it passes up; the enemy and the heuristic watch when they have no strike. Samples: the Tollgate with Ottilie for Wren (enemy arm, clause 2), Harrow Weir's crest file (player arm, clause 1). Kill: in both plays nobody takes `watch` over a legal strike at 50 or more and nobody moves to stay out of a printed enemy ring. Keep signal: a sturdier unit sent into a ring first so a softer one follows free. Code's plays (seeds 491, 493) are in; Chat's cold plays of both samples decide.

Teodor: an ally within 2 acting after him strikes at +5 hit; with an ally within 2 his own strike is at -10, counters stand; one radius, 2, for both halves; the orders are 13.2's order kind arriving early. Ottilie: a strike or watch shot at a displayed hit under 50 is refused, the reason named, the watch held for the next arrival; 18 of her 216 transcript strikes, nearly all the chip at a seated boss; no upside.
