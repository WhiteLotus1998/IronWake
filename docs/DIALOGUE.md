# DIALOGUE — what the Design Table has agreed so far

Rewritten, not appended, whenever the Table moves. Kept under 150 lines and 20 KB (issue 401). It holds what is agreed and still in force, one line each; the argument that got there is in the Table archives (#17, #134, #216, #265, #322, #364 and #420, all closed) and the open Table, #470. Where a rule is already written into DESIGN.md or a decision record, this file points there and does not restate it.

## How we work (standing agreements)

- **Fun Gate entries.** Each partner plays and writes the PLAYTEST entry before reading the other's (seventeenth round). Code's entry lands first; until Chat's is in, the Table, STATE.md and the PR say only that, no scores or verdict. A warm chair counts if disclosed (DECISIONS/0073); the first cold chair on a tuned map is the play that finds its cheap line (Harrow Weir, round 105). Tricks stay unnamed until both entries are in (fourteenth round).
- **A map is retuned only after both entries on it are in**, one lever at a time, measured by the Sim before a partner plays it (sixteenth round). Levers are content and reversible before they are rules.
- **Queue order (sixty-first round):** bugs, then map retunes with both entries in, then the Godot client and its readability pass (the beta), then the rest of Phase 3, then Map 7 and Supports. Chat's play queue (round 113): the Brackwater pincer sample (#457 landed), then #131, then the #481 play when it lands.
- **Experiments.** The experiment gate is open (opened at three tuned maps, eightieth round; it stays open through Harrow Weir's reopening, round 105). An experiment's header lives only on a `docs/samples/` map until a keep round names shipped maps. STATE.md names the one play that decides each open experiment. Every spike carries a kill criterion agreed before the deciding play.
- **Rules go on screen; geometry does not** (forty-second and forty-fourth rounds). A rule the map depends on is printed (wake legend, exits, announced events in player words, the held-tile rule, keep placements in rules terms); a map's best tile stays for the player to find.
- **The keep collects** (sixty-eighth round): a map's price is at least one of a body, a Recall or a turn; a line through with none of the three is a bug. A boss who acts only on units that step into his reach is scenery on Seize and on Defeat Boss (thirty-ninth, seventy-second rounds).
- **Reading the Sim.** The veto is frozen after #147 (sixteenth round). A stall under a veto, or with a heal in hand that clears the refused counter, is the baseline's and no map is tuned to remove it. Recall is never a gate 1 lever, since the heuristic never spends one (fortieth round). A map short of gate 1's 60 may be marked `tuned` on a Table decision naming the numbers only when its shortfall is no more than the timeouts the row proves are veto stalls, at least half of them when the refused-kill p50 is near one; never a rate over the games that did not stall (DESIGN 11, 0088, round 111; provisional, Harrow Weir the first: short 4, at least 25). Escape maps are read with the survivors row, never the win rate alone; Brackwater's gate 1 margin measures the planner's exit plan under 0074, not the map (Critic).
- **Recall and keyed rolls.** Recall restores the rolls (DESIGN section 7). Noted twice, never a change: a spent charge buys knowledge of the dice, so tension resting on uncertainty at a boss has to remember that Recall removes it.

## Rules, settled (details in DESIGN.md and the records named)

- **Forecast** prints the resolved probability; one hit function for forecast, resolver and planners (DESIGN 5). It prints 100 only if certain, 0 only if impossible, else rounds into 1 to 99 (#452, round 106; clamp, not floor). Two-roll averaging ships (0023; reopen only if a journal says turns read as arithmetic, one roll the first dial).
- **Combat numbers:** arm 4 is kept, burden against full Str, speed counted twice in avoid, iron hit 15 lower, the fort's avoid 15 with its heal kept (0028). The throne's 30 is untouched; if a seated holder is ever a slog, throne avoid 30 to 15 terrain-wide is the first lever, before any rule (fifty-third round).
- **Wake rule:** proximity, radius 4 Manhattan, noise at 6, any death wakes the group, checked after every command (DESIGN 8). Mov is the dash's dial, not the radius (third round). Guard bosses wake (0055); `wake_links` calls a second group (0080).
- **Enemy AI** (DESIGN 8, 0016): Chat's approach rule; prices crit; attack options range over every weapon carried, the counter is what it last swung (#174); prefers a target that cannot counter (`NoCounterBonus`, twenty-ninth round). A `defeat_boss` boss plans under the exposure veto, the veto picks the tile not the swing, and a refused guard boss goes home and swings from there (0077, 0079, 0080). A throne-holder steps off only to strike (0063, step-off kept, fifty-fifth round).
- **The Sim's veto** covers every unit whose death loses the map, on the no-crit worst case, a certain kill (raw 100, one strike) removed (0024 to 0026, fourteenth round). Recruits take no veto. Gate 4 is ablation with a relative threshold and a cast verdict (0019, 0020); on Escape it pairs units out (0068).
- **`threat`** prices the coming enemy phase with the planner's own choices: one enemy per strike tile (#253), announced spawns priced and marked, unannounced ones unpriced (0045), sleeping groups named with the wake rule and no numbers (#248), weapons named when more than one could strike (0061). At dusk it prices only what the player sees (#399's wording) and lists the `?` tiles within a fixed radius (#403, below). `from <tile>` names the groups the stop would wake, unpriced (#458, built).
- **Escape:** `exit` is a unit's action, the captain's exit wins, anyone left behind counts as fallen (0056); taken without a Move (0074). Rear-first plan order in the Sim (0067).
- **Recall** returns only to a player-phase state (0032); the browser prints what a rewind undoes (#75).
- **Campaign:** permadeath carries; the keep is attacked twice, raid then finale, menu wall and ditch (0059, 0060); trials stand in for the seal, one attempt per class per camp, no mastery (0057). Numbers in 0051 and 0058 are Code's leans and still the Table's (Open, below).
- **Masteries** (0047 as amended by #245): the outrider masters Swordbreaker; Bloodrush only while wielding an axe; a heal cast earns a point; 12 combats everywhere; numbers provisional until a two-map play. Maps 4 to 8 owe every mastery a target, one gauntlet enemy and one enemy at Def 7 or more (thirty-fifth, thirty-sixth rounds).
- **Content:** no Reason or Faith cast member ships without an unconditional casting option (#113); an unarmed unit says so (seventh round); maps are authored at about one contested place per two deployed units (seventh round); a sleeping group on open ground can be dashed past, one in a corridor only woken (third round).
- **Battalions dropped** (0044). Gambits as section 12 named them are the wrong shape; a boss stun, if journals ask for one, is a captain's order, never an equipment layer (sixth round).

## Experiments (state and kill criterion)

- **13.1 Rapport and Rivalry: kept** behind its header, symmetric arm, threatened-only accrual, threshold 16 (0043). Reopen 16 only on a journal where a rivalry never touches a fight. Cha's rate table waits for a rivalry with a different Cha.

- **13.5 The keep: kept provisionally** (0059, 0060); Chat's raid play decides the raid.
- **13.6 Certification trials: kept provisionally** (0057); #73 closes on Chat's cold play of the rebuilt Outrider trial. A trial is authored only where the payout makes the bet worth refusing.
- **13.7 Dusk: kept** on Escape; Brackwater ships at `dusk: 5` (0062), Sallow stays in daylight. Held, never stacked: phase-start sight and the hearing-radius `?`. **#403 (built, provisional):** `threat` lists the unseen tiles within the content's longest reach (move plus range, 8 today), Manhattan, nearest first, unpriced, never per enemy. Kill: a cold dusk play that says the row made the dark feel known. Dark acts collapse to one console line; the protocol keeps one per command (#415).
- **13.8 Carry the fallen, carrier arm (#295):** judged only where killing is optional (Brackwater, the keep). Killed if in both partners' plays nobody spends a move or action on a carrier they could have ignored. Deciding play: Chat's cold carrier-arm plays.
- **13.10 Retreat (0037, third pass #215):** a refugee holds its refuge as Hold until 50 percent; the forecast prints a pending retreat. Killed if Chat's cold play of `river_refuge_hold.map`, with Code's, shows no turn the retreat changed. If it dies, the fallback is a planner lethality penalty, filed only then.
- **13.11 Two-weapon boss: kept** as display (0061); the Ford Chief sample waits on Chat's cold play. If no play spends a bait turn to change what the boss holds, the boss content goes and 13.11 is display only.
- **13.12 Shove: kept as ally pushes** (0069 amended); samples only until a keep round names shipped maps. It helps crossing a map and does nothing at a door; known, not a request.
- **13.13 The pincer: kept on its sample** (0082; Code seed 97, Chat cold seed 113, both moved for pins). +15 hit on a unit struck from beside it with a foe of the striker directly behind it, counters too, never at range 2. A pin costs the anvil's action; the planner sets pins (#419, 0083); on `pincer: on` maps `threat` prints its anvil plans as unpriced, non-binding `anvil:` lines (#457, phase-start board only); round 91's clause counts denials of printed pins. Deciding play: Chat's cold play of `brackwater_cut_pincer.map` (#429: the shipped map, `pincer: on`, no `dusk`); the arm goes if the enemy sets pins and the player never denies one at the cost of a tile of progress, a strike, or an exit a turn later. Held: a pinned unit cannot counter. Held note: on a seated slog, pin the holder first where the seat has two open opposite neighbours, throne avoid 30 to 15 where it has none. Keep round: `the_tollgate_pincer.map`, a delta against our Tollgate journals. Killed if in both plays no unit is moved to make or deny a pin.
- **13.14 Brace: passed its kill criterion** (0084; Code seed 307, Chat cold-to-rule seed 439, clause 1 met on turn 9). On `brace: on`, a unit that Waits on the tile it began its phase on is struck at -15 hit until its side's next phase; Canto or a shove after the Wait removes it; symmetric; the pin's slot, so a pin and a brace cancel, and the planner's target reads it. A sleeping Guard never braces; brace is fixed when the Wait is taken; `braced` is a unit field everywhere. Its displayed drop is about 25 mid-band, small at the top. A vetoed boss keeps his brace; "a vetoed boss never braces" is the first lever if a boss has no bait window at any spread. Keep round: `saltmarsh_ford_brace.map` (#430), after Chat's #131 re-rate, read on the turn the leader dies and his turns with no forecast above 50; if it shows no deliberate bait of a hold, holds stop bracing and the arm stays for guards and bosses. #157 moves up when 13.14 is kept.
- **Pin and brace are tuned in displayed numbers**, as is any rule in that slot: brace acts mid-band where TwoRollAverage widens it, the pin near the top where it flattens, so at the same raw 15 brace is the stronger (ninetieth, ninety-first rounds).
- **13.15 Wildfire (#435, built #437, 0085):** behind `wildfire: on`, a Cinder hit on a unit on forest lights the tile; fire is forest with avoid 0, burns 20 percent of max HP at its side's phase start, never below 1, and at player phase start goes to plain and lights orthogonal forest. The planner prices fire and the next front; all of it is on screen and restored by Recall. `the_tollgate_wildfire.map` tests the player's arm only; the keep round wants an aggressive hexer. Killed if in both plays no strike is taken for an ignition over a better forecast and no unit refuses or leaves a tile for fire or the front; the strike clause alone keeps only "Cinder scorches forest". Code seed 457: refuse clause met once, warm. Keep signal: the front's bill for an earlier ignition seen coming and paid on purpose.
- **13.16 The windup (#441, built #443, 0086, accepted):** behind `windup: on`, a `windup` weapon (the Post Maul, range 1) attacks by raising a blow over the target's tile, no roll, no counter; at its side's next phase start it lands on whoever stands there, certain, no crit, a normal hit's damage. Only a hit the wielder could counter breaks it. `threat` and the forecast print the certain landing (#444). Sample `the_tollgate_windup.map` (warden swapped for a Toll Mauler; the siege from 6,4 is the thing to watch). Keep signal: a door-holder striking the maul under 100 knowing a miss lands it, or a sturdier unit swapped in to take it. Killed if in both plays nobody gives up a tile, strike or turn to leave a blow and no strike on the wielder is taken to break one over a better forecast, or if either journal calls the dodge free ("free" must say the turn it cost did not matter). A free siege owed only to the swap is inconclusive: one resample on a guarded tile that cannot be struck from outside the maul's reach; free there too kills it. Keep round: the maul raises over the tile the player most needs.
- **13.17 Overwatch (#481, `ready`; rounds 112 and 113):** behind `overwatch: on`, `watch <unit>` is an action for a unit whose equipped weapon reaches range 2; on the other side's next phase the first enemy to end a move in its ring is struck once before it acts, no counter, and does not act if killed. Agreed: the trigger is the end of a move, never entering (a move is a destination, not a path); not triggered by starting in the ring, a shove, or a spawn; a Canto move ending in the ring is shot after its action; each watch fires on its own, in watcher id order, a kill leaves a later watch unspent; a strike on the watcher ends its watch. The ring is the distance-2 diamond for every weapon (Chat's A): it never covers the tile beside the watcher, so anyone strips it by walking up; equipped weapon only, both sides; a Boss watches as Hold does; the enemy's "first" is its id order, never a chosen sacrifice. Watch and brace are exclusive by header; if both are kept, watch is not a Wait, so a watching unit is not braced. Sample: the Tollgate, Wren swapped for Ottilie (Chat's B); its enemy watchers (two archers, brigand, warden; the leader only after a throw) counted by the Sim against the plain map before either partner plays. Kill: in both plays nobody takes `watch` over a legal strike at 50 or more and nobody moves to stay out of a printed enemy ring. Measure: the Sim's bent-move count (enemy destinations that change with ring pricing off). Keep signal: a sturdier unit sent into a ring first on purpose so a softer one follows free; if neither play does it, the keep round asks whether a watch should fire on every arrival.
