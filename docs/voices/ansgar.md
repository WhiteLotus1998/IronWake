# Ansgar, the outrider

**Rule 1: stands.** Ansgar may name a feeling once, as a scene's turn, and the beat sheet names it in advance (below).

**Who.** Sallow, fen-born; a courier sold as a boy to Hask's companies, eighteen, an outrider, met on the field (map 9), last of anyone. He ran the night before his swearing, on the horse they gave him for it. Wants to stay free, and to stop running. Won't say how far the warmth had got.

**Image source** (rule 5). The fen behind Sallow's counting houses: reed beds, a punt pole, mud that keeps a boot, mist. The courier's road: the satchel, a seal he never broke, the next post. Never ledgers, tolls or tides (Ottilie's; her Sallow is the canal's front, his the fen behind it). Never fire, as an image or a comfort: he won't reach for it.

**Shape.** Quick and half-finished, verbs of going. He answers a question on his way out of the conversation, or answers the next one instead (rule 4). He talks most from the saddle. He has the fewest lines of anyone, so each one is short.

**Wit:** yes, about his own running. Never about the swearing.

**Names** (rule 8). Others' names quickly, the way a courier learns a route. His own late and to few: to a stranger he is "the rider". The horse has a name Hask's men gave it, and he won't say it aloud.

**The one named feeling:** proposed for the camp after the field (map 9), the one night he stays. Scared, said once, about staying. The beat sheet confirms it.

**Never.**
- "free", "freedom" (he says "out", "gone", "clear")
- "warm", except in his line about the swearing
- fire, hearth, a lit window
- **Most at risk:** the running joke on every line. One a scene.
- **Second risk:** the aphorism. His placeholder warmth line ("It's like coming in from the cold...") is one; it is his one.

**Samples** (illustrative; they don't ship and carry no ids).
1. `bark`, a long move, to nobody: "Gone. Keep up."
2. `bark`, wildfire on the board, to nobody: "Not that way. Any way but that."
3. `support`, to Ottilie, after "That horse is worth more than you are.": "Then buy us both. He won't go anywhere without me."
4. `support`, to Keziah, who has complained about the cold: "Fen's colder. Wetter too. You'd hate it. I'll show you round."
5. `support`, to Maud, who wants him by the fire to be stitched: "Stitch me here. I can see the needle fine."
6. `scene`, the field, near Hollin's sworn: "Don't stand near them long. It gets in through your hands."
7. `card`, his meeting, the field: "A boy on a good horse, riding the wrong way through Hask's lines. He doesn't stop to explain. He stops when the captain does."

Sample 5 is the flaw without the word: the fire is the thing he's running from, and nobody in the scene says so.

**Average test.** Stock: "I'm done running. I'll stand and fight with you!" Ours: "I'll stay till morning. Ask me again in the morning."
