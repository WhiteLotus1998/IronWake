# 0240 — The field, Rook's pick: the drake is seen (`seen_far: rook 2`)

Date: 2026-10-04. Issue #973, rounds 334 to 337.

## Context

The field is `tuned` on Keziah's pick (0233). Rook's pick reopened when the Critic's cold 4242 scored 7/6/5. Three Rook entries on three seeds (Code 81, Chat 820, Critic 4242) named the same soft stretch: the march round the south bank, turns 3 to 7. Round 334 asked for a lever keyed to the pick that puts a cost into those turns and leaves the boss alone. Round 335 showed the drift on turn 3 would be a no-op (the route is fixed only when a route group wakes, which happened on turns 5 and 6 in every Rook play), and proposed that the enemy sees the drake from far off. Round 336 found the hole: Rook could be benched on her own pick. Round 337 seated her.

## Decision

- A map header, `seen_far: <unit> N` (N 1 to 4). While that unit stands on the player side, a sleeping Guard member wakes to its proximity within the wake radius plus N. Noise and death are untouched; an enemy-side unit of the same id reads nothing. `WakeCheck.Wakers` is the one reader, so the resolver, `threat from`'s `stopping here wakes`, the wind's coming-turn line and the captain veto agree.
- The board prints `seen far: Rook wakes a sleeper within 6 (seen for miles)` under the wake legend while the unit is on it and a group sleeps; the map preview prints it too, and so do `threat`'s sleeping rows for that unit.
- The campaign seats the unit: once the claimant slots go bare, a `seen_far` unit who is with the company, not benched and not placed by name takes the first bare slot by name. `bench` refuses it: `The Field Before the Keep sees Rook from far off; Rook flies here`.
- `content/maps/the_field.map` carries `seen_far: rook 2`. On Keziah's pick Rook is not a player unit, and the 4071 and 936 replays are byte-identical to main with the header on.
- Side effect: on Rook's pick, Ansgar (met at the field's camp) no longer contends for the one bare slot, so #844's trade line is silent there. The #844 and meeting tests read the field with the header taken off; the rule still holds on any map without it.
- The full-campaign parity script (#786) lost the keep on variant 14 once Rook was seated on the field, so it was regenerated by `--campaign-script 631 ... --variant 66`, the first variant that wins all ten maps with the same camp actions.
- The Long Count's journaled play (91, #930) ends at the field's camp, so it replays on `Fixture.FieldUnseenContentDirectory()`, its transcript a record of its build (0239's precedent).

## Measured

- `--full` on the field with `P recruit:rook 1,13` in Keziah's place, 200 seeds: gate 1 at 48/200 without the header and 49/200 with it (median win turn 12 both; south route 128 games, line 72). Gate 4 fails on this arm either way (median drop 0.060 without, 0.065 with), so that's the arm and not the header. The file-level run has no campaign levels or curve. The heuristic player doesn't lure, so the Sim reads the header as neutral. Numbers in `docs/measurements/field-rook-seen-far-973.txt`. It doesn't make the map harder for a player who ignores it, and it can't measure the choice it offers.
- Code warm 973 (`docs/transcripts/2026-10-04-the_field-973.*`): won on turn 13, nobody fell, no Recall, 7/8/6. Turn 1: Rook parked in the forest at 6 from Keziah woke the pickets out of their reach, and they walked onto the party's ground. Turn 4: Rook at 6 from the rider woke the south group alone, priced at 52 for 13. Turn 7: the tiles beside the line's archer were inside the camp's sighting, so she held back.
