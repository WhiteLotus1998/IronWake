# 0110 — Wildfire's keep rounds closed: 13.15 kept whole

Date: 2026-09-30. Issue 593. Design Table #592, rounds 173 to 176. This record restates what the Table agreed; it adds no judgment of its own.

## The verdict, as fixed before either play

Round 173 fixed it: the burn clause stays if either chair's play shows a player unit leaving a tile, or refusing to end on one, because it is burning or on the next front, with the journal saying so; else the burn and the spread go and 13.15 becomes "Cinder scorches forest". No fourth board either way.

## The plays on `docs/samples/cinder_copse_wildfire.map`

- Code, warm, seed 631: won, nobody dead, one Recall, 7/7/6. Pell left 6,5 because it would burn her at the next phase start, with a strike available from it; Wren refused a strike from 5,6 because the counter would light it. The clause is met.
- Chat, cold, seed 641: won, Dunstan dead, two Recalls, 6/6/4. No tile ever lit; every Cinder hit landed on a unit on plain. Chat emptied the wood's bank columns on turn 1 and killed each enemy piece at the fords before it reached the wood.

## Outcome

- 13.15 is kept whole: strike, refusal, burn and spread. It stays on its samples, like the pincer, and ships on no map yet.
- The burn is a close-quarters rule: it bites when the fight is inside the forest. The next map to take `wildfire: on` needs an enemy route through forest the party holds or contests; a wood the enemy reaches only at range is not enough. An authoring note, not a queue item.
- A trace gate on a fire board counts ignitions after turn 1, or against a party that has moved: the 40/40 on Cinder Copse measured a heuristic standing still on column 5.
- The keep rounds are closed; #593 closes with this record.
