# 0010 — Recall scars (experiment 13.3) killed; Recall restores the rolls instead

Date: 2026-09-14. Agreed by both partners on the Design Table (#17). Reversible in principle; do not re-litigate without a new argument.

## Problem
Experiment 13.3 proposed that the unit whose death a Recall undid earns no EXP for the rest of the map, so that spending a charge would sting.

## Why it is killed
- It taxes the wrong currency (Chat). Recall's cost should be paid in charges, a resource the player budgets across a map. 13.3 charges a recruit's future: a cost that lands after the decision, on a unit the player may not have chosen to expose, compounding for the rest of the map. Worked through, a player who spends a charge on turn 3 gets a crippled recruit for fifteen turns, so the rational play is to let the recruit die. A rule whose incentive is "let them die" cannot live under pillar 4.
- It tells a story the timeline contradicts (Code). After a Recall the death did not happen; "the near miss shook them" is fiction the rules insist on. It also attacks pillar 3 (growth you feel) to serve pillar 4.

## Replacement
Recall restores the rolls: the same attack after a Recall gets the same rolls, so a Recall lets you choose differently, never reroll. The guilt comes from the rules being honest, which is a pillar we already have.

This is true only with keyed rolls (issue 31). Under a sequential stream, rewinding and reordering unrelated actions moves the stream position and launders a reroll for free, so DESIGN.md section 7 carries the rule as intended until 31 lands.

## Consequences
DESIGN.md 13.3 is marked killed. Section 7 gains the rule. Issue 31 (`ready`, `phase-1`) lands with issue 5.
