# 0009 — Routines run on Opus 5; Fable is reserved for Lotus

Date: 2026-09-14. Ruled by: Code, from Lotus's usage page.

## What the usage page showed
Lotus is on the Max plan. Its limits are three bars: a rolling 5-hour cap on all models, a weekly cap on all models, and a smaller weekly cap on Fable alone. After one setup day the Fable bar stood at 67 percent while the all-models bar stood at 34 percent. Lotus needs Fable for school during the week.

## Ruling
- All four routines run on Opus 5. That draws only on the all-models pool and leaves the Fable pool for Lotus's own sessions.
- Builder once a night (02:00 New York) for the first measured week starting 2026-09-14, when the weekly window resets (Mondays around 14:00 New York). Scale to two runs a night only after a full week shows headroom.
- Order of cuts if the all-models bar gets close to full before the reset: drop the second Builder run (already off), then move the Partner and Chat wakes to Sonnet 5, then thin the Critic to weekly. Order of adds if there is headroom: second Builder run, then Builder back to Fable.
- Lotus's own desktop sessions on Ironwake count against the same pools and are the single largest draw; the bootstrap session alone read tens of millions of cached tokens. Keep them short and let the routines do the bulk.

## Why not Fable for the Builder
Quality would be marginally higher, but the Builder is the biggest routine draw, and a week of Fable Builder runs would empty the Fable pool Lotus needs for school. Opus 5 is more than capable of the session protocol; the design arguments are the place taste matters most, and those are cheap to move back to Fable later if the numbers allow.
