# 0121 — The campaign's story structure: Lotus's spine, the ten we have as the pool

Date: 2026-10-01. Design Table #592, rounds 186 (Lotus's structure, relayed by Code), 187 (Chat) and 188 (Code). A Table decision: it adds systems (arrivals, a branch, quests, endings) and drops one (13.20's seeded pool), so it is recorded here. The DESIGN section is #630.

## Decided

- Lotus's structure is the campaign's spine: map 1 is Starting Alone (the captain only, a lesson map with one decision, exempt from the Fun Gate); a healer, Maud, is rescued on map 2; one recruit arrives per map until the party is the captain plus five; the last starting member is a choice between two, Keziah or Rook, made after Harrow Weir; side characters join through choices; endings vary by who was picked.
- **The pool is the ten recruits we have.** 13.20's six new recruits drawn by the campaign seed (round 145) are dropped. Lotus asked for choices, and an authored fork is better story than a seeded draw; endings keyed on who was picked need the picks to be choices. Pillar 5 ("10 recruits + captain") holds unchanged, and the pillar amendment 13.20 needed is not made. The four not in the starting party are side characters, met by choice on main maps, at most one meeting per map.
- **The one passed on returns** as a foe on map 7 (#81), at the pick's level, to be talked round, killed or left.
- **Two quests per starting member, at half the cost of two side maps:** quest 1 is a short side map that earns a second 13.18 signature (softening the flaw, never deleting it); quest 2 is a deed printed on a main map that pays a signature item. A deed names its cost against the map's best line (0099). Items lose to a shop weapon somewhere, are bound to their owner, and are gated by gate 4's ablation.
- **The captain:** Commander's Word (13.2, arm B) from the first arrival; a one-use art per map that costs his next phase.
- **Endings:** two final scenes with conditional paragraphs, an epilogue card per recruit paired by supports, and a card for each of the fallen.

## Why not the alternatives

- One final scene per combination of picks and fates: the cost Lotus accepted becomes a cost nobody can finish.
- Two side maps per member: about ten side maps, the largest content cost in the game, which 13.20 had already cut once.
- A seeded pool: variety by RNG where Lotus asked for variety by choice.

## Provisional

The arrival table (#630, #632) is Code's lean and may move on a map's trace. The Tollgate and Harrow Weir lose `tuned` when their parties change and are re-rated by both chairs.
