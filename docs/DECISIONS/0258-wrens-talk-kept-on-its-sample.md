- **A second band.** The diagonal 9,4, 10,5, 11,6, 12,7 lies 7 or 8 from a sleeping fort member and outside 6 of all of them, so a Wren fight there wakes the fort early. Chat's turn-4 soldier stopped on 10,5. The pair's band and the fort's do not touch; 8,4 to 10,6 is quiet to both. The Sim's 200-seed sweep cannot count how often the ford fight lands in the fort's band: its heuristic wakes the fort by proximity on turn 2 in every seed. A hand play is the measure, as for the pair.
- **Campaign tripwire:** if no campaign journal through Wren's quests names a turn where the talk kept her from a swing and that cost something (a worse striker, a survivor, a wasted turn), it returns to the Table as a candidate for a quest-1 softening, not a kill.
