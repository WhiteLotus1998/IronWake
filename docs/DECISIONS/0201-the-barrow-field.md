## What is built

- **The lance from arrival.** `units/cast.json` gives Teodor `iron_lance`, `field_dressing`, `family_lance`, in that order. The iron is equipped, so every map he stands on is fought as before unless the player swings the lance. It goes third, not second, so a script's `item teodor 2` is still the dressing. The forecast now names the weapon on Teodor's lines (`with Iron Lance`), because he carries two, and 24 transcripts were regenerated for that text alone. No outcome in any of them moved.
- **The hold.** A ladder's optional `holdsAt` names a stage before the last (`HeirloomLadder.HoldsAt`). A quest's optional `wakes` names an heirloom bound to its member whose ladder holds, at most one quest per heirloom, and the loader refuses anything else. `CampaignRecord.Held` lists every heirloom whose waking quest the record has not won, and `Begin` and `BeginQuest` put it on the battle (`BattleState.Held`, the protocol's `held`), so the hold applies on main maps and side maps alike, the waking quest's own board included. On a held battle the combat still counts, but the stage never turns past the hold. Outside the campaign nothing is held.
- **The waking.** On a won `wakes` quest, `AfterQuest` turns the stack one stage when it stands at the hold with the count already at the next threshold, and the after line says `Family Lance wakes in Teodor's hands`. Otherwise the stack is unchanged, and the next combat that reaches 10 turns it as usual, since the record no longer holds it.
- **The card.** At the hold, with the count past the next threshold and the hold applying, Teodor's card adds `(the rust holds; it waits on Teodor)` (pillar 2: the gate is printed, never a silent stop). The counter is still never printed.
- **The board.** `content/quests/the_barrow_field.map`: defeat_boss, 14x9, limit 8, Recall 2, enemy level 4, `announce: on`. The sworn captain stands at the barrow mouth, a hill at 10,4 walled to the east. Jory (a soldier) digs at 9,4, bound to him by `freed: 9,4 by dig`. An archer is on the hill at 10,2 and a brigand on 12,7, all of them the dig group, asleep. A brigand comes up the west road on turn 2 and a soldier on turn 3, announced. Teodor holds the captain slot at 1,4, and the ally's bare slot is at 1,5. The twist is that his brother fights him until the captain falls: Jory has 21 HP, the lance at sound counters him for 12 or 13 and the iron for 10, so which lance Teodor carries in front is a choice about his brother as well as about damage. `campaign.json`'s `teodor_1`: part 1, `opensAfter: harrow_weir` (Teodor is on the roster from the start, so arrival timing would open it after map 1), `wakes: family_lance`, 2 common, appended so no other side map's seed moves. The cards are placeholders until the writing pass. The board is named for the field, not the sergeant, because the Postern's hidden class is the Sergeant and no listing names it before the Postern is won.
