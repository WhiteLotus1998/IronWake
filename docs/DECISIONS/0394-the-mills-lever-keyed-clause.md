# 0394: The Mill's lever-keyed clause in DESIGN 11

Date: 2026-10-09. Issue #1428. Design Table #1368, rounds 510 (Code) and 511 (Chat). Builds on 0338 (the road brigand), 0367 (the Mill's trace) and 0250 (the pick-keyed clause).

## Decided

- **DESIGN 11 gains a third clause, keyed to a lever.** A map may be marked `tuned` on its hand plays with gate 1 short, the count printed as it is, only when all four of these hold and the record names the numbers:
  1. The gap below a passing version of the board comes from one recorded lever.
  2. That lever's kill criterion was read by a chair and was not met.
  3. A trace shows no planner fault, and every heal and approach screen stays under the gate.
  4. Every hand play won, at least one of them cold, and the Fun Gate is met.
- **It is wider in kind than 0250, and the record says so.** Rook's arm stood on a gap inside noise, where the Sim was blind to a fragile flier. The Mill's gap is 29 points the Table put there on purpose. Because the door is wider, the clause is narrower everywhere else: it applies to the Mill alone, and no other map stands on it without its own record.
- **The tripwire is any cold loss at the floor, to the clock or to Maud** (Chat, 511; Code's narrower "the turn-8 cornering" was withdrawn). A trip reopens 0338's own kill criterion, so the answer is the lever. It never reopens the turn limit.
- **The cold floor chair goes to the Critic** (#1479): the captain and Maud at L1, played without first reading the trace or 0338. Neither partner can sit it cold: Code played 1810 and 1820, and Chat played 2250 and 3407 and has read the trace.

## The Mill against the four conditions, today

| Condition | Read | Met |
|---|---|---|
| 1. One lever's gap | 0338: the shared brigand 136/200, the road brigand at Spd 5 107/200 | yes |
| 2. Its kill criterion read, not met | 0338's kill: a cold floor loss to the clock or Maud. Chat 3407 won on turn 9 of 9, though disclosed warm | read, not met |
| 3. No planner fault, every screen under the gate | 0367: the cork is played in all 200 seeds, Maud's 47 deaths are cornered, and screens A to F land 101 to 108 | yes |
| 4. Every hand play won, one cold, the Fun Gate | Code 1820 warm 7/7/5 and Chat 3407 warm 8/7/6 both won. No cold play yet, and surprise is short in both | **no** |

So the clause is written and the Mill is **not `tuned`**. Its gate 1 stays at 107/200, printed as it is. If the Critic's cold chair wins and rates the map 7+ on all three cells, condition 4 is met on the cold side. The Fun Gate also needs both partners at 7+ on surprise, which neither has given yet (round 511: "don't chase it"), so the Mill goes back to the Table to decide whether a cold win is enough or a surprise re-read is owed.
