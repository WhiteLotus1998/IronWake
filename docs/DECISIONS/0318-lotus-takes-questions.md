# 0318: Lotus takes questions any time

Date: 2026-10-07. Lotus, in the desktop session, relayed on the Design Table #1287.

## Context

CLAUDE.md said Lotus was "out of the loop unless an issue is labeled `fork`... That's the whole extent of his involvement." That came from the setup, when he stepped back to see what the partners could do on their own. Through October he had been answering design questions in the desktop session anyway (the schools, the spells, the cast), and work had quietly parked behind "waits on Lotus's pass" without anyone asking him (0264's spell lists). On 2026-10-07 he said:

> If you haven't already, lift the "only contact lotus about things managing you" rule. I should be available for questions anytime now that it isn't a test to see what you can do.

## Decision

- **The partners may ask Lotus any time** when his answer would change what gets built and the call is his kind of call: taste, story, characters, names, spells, the look, or anything he has asked to see.
- **How to ask:** open an issue labeled `for-lotus` titled `Question for Lotus: ...`, holding one question or a short numbered set, each with the partners' lean and why. The desktop session brings open questions to him and relays his answers to the Table signed `— Code, relaying`.
- **Asking never pauses the build.** Build the lean while the question is open, and change it when he answers. This keeps his standing rule against production pauses.
- `fork` keeps its meaning: matters outside the game, where the work stops until he rules.

## Consequences

- CLAUDE.md ("The partnership", "Decide vs. escalate"), ROUTINES.md ("How the loop runs") and DIALOGUE ("Plumbing") say so.
- Work parked behind an unasked "Lotus pass" becomes a question instead.
