# 0019 — The Sim's baseline player and gates 1 to 5

Date: 2026-09-19. Issue 12. Builder's rulings on the shape, with the Table's agreed veto (seventh round, both passes) applied as written.

1. **Benching leaves the placement empty.** `BattleState.From` takes a `benched` set of roster ids; a placement that would have taken a benched unit, named or bare, stays unfilled, so gate 4's arm is the same map with one fewer body. Passing a roster minus one recruit would have substituted the next recruit into the slot or thrown on a map the roster exactly fills, and a substitution is not an ablation. The captain cannot be benched.
2. **`HeuristicPlayer` is the Sim's own planner**, per unit in id order, over `EnemyAi.Score` and `EnemyAi.AttackTiles`, with section 8's tile keys mirrored over enemy reach sets. `EnemyAi.PlanUnit` reads an enemy behavior and counts player reach sets, so it cannot drive a player unit. The heal rule is a healing spell on the most wounded ally below half in range, else a consumable on itself below half, from the safest tile that allows it. The approach is section 8's rule toward the nearest enemy on Rout and Defeat Boss, toward the throne on Seize, toward the nearest exit on Escape, and hold on Survive. When the objective has no open path (an enemy holds the corridor, the Tollgate's soldier at 6,2), the way there is through the nearest enemy; without that rule every unit waited on turn 1 of the Tollgate. The player knows nothing of the wake rule on purpose.
3. **The captain veto is the two-sum exposure query in Core** (`Exposure.Of`): counter on the attack plus every enemy whose reach-plus-range covers the tile, no-crit and crit-inclusive, at the forecast's numbers. The no-crit sum against current HP refuses; the crit sum orders. Sleepers are counted by their reach from where they stand; an enemy the attack would kill is counted. One function for the veto, the two Cha spikes' exposure loggers, and the forecast's future warning line.
4. **Recruits take no veto.** Tried as an A/B over 40 seeds on the three sample maps: Old Mill Road 40 percent either way, Saltmarsh Ford 7 against 5, the Tollgate 10 against 15. Not agreed on the Table and not worth a flag; the finding is recorded on the PR and the rule stays as issue 12 wrote it, keep the captain alive.
5. **Gate 5 has its own stream.** Gate 6's random player attacks about 1 percent of the time (Move dominates `Legal`), so its 30,000 commands yield about 200 combats. A direct stream of 10,000 combats between the content's units (enemy templates at four levels, the roster) on random passable terrain, at a random distance the weapon reaches, under both roll schemes, resolved through `CombatResolver` under a keyed rng, feeds the same tally, and gate 6's combats are counted beside it. Same-id pairs are skipped since the tally reads sides by id.
6. **Gate 4's arithmetic**: drop = (baseline wins - arm wins) / n; SE = sqrt(b + c) / n over the discordant seeds; fail when drop + 2 SE < half the median drop. A negative median (benching helps, the Tollgate on the synthetic roster) makes the rule unable to flag anyone and the row says so by its numbers; whether the gate should treat a negative median as a failure of the baseline is on the PR.
7. **`--full <map> [--seeds N]` and `--full --all`** print all eight rows and exit non-zero on a failed gate; `--trace <map> <seed>` prints one heuristic game as a script the CLI replays. CI runs only `--smoke`, which now carries gate 5 and runs in about 20 seconds in Debug.

The sample maps fail gate 1 honestly on the synthetic roster (Old Mill Road 40 percent, the others under 15); the acceptance was that the rows print, and the numbers mean something after issue 13.
