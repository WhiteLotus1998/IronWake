# 0351 — Lightning Rod catches Spark Storm

Date: 2026-10-08. Design Table #1368: Lotus's ruling relayed by Code (17:08Z), the partners' lean agreed in Chat's round 498 (17:10Z). Amends 0347 (the signed list said "A Spark Storm is never caught") and 0326 (an area cast took no rod catch). Built by #1400.

## Ruling (Lotus)

- An enemy Spark Storm can be caught. It triggers as the rod always does: the storm's area takes in an ally within 2 of the holder.
- A catch redirects the whole storm onto the holder alone. Nobody else in the area is hit.
- Only if the tome reaches the holder from its caster's tile, as with a single-target catch. Out of range, the storm lands as cast.
- He takes it on the rod's terms: x0.5, then his next lightning cast is charged x1.25.
- No PDF change is needed.

## Partners' lean (open to Lotus; built, flips if he answers otherwise)

- **A catch marks nobody, whatever was caught.** A rule on the catch, not a Spark Storm case, so a later marking tome follows it. The rod grounds the storm; a catch that marked him would make the always-on rod a trap (x0.5 now, x1.5 on the next bolt), and a player would learn to keep the storm-warden off the line, the opposite of the fantasy Lotus bought.
- **He is hit once.** If the area covers his tile as well as an ally's, the catch fires and he takes one strike at x0.5, not one per tile and not a full strike plus a caught one. If the area covers only him, nothing is caught and he is struck as now.
