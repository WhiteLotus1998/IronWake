# 0024 — The exposure sum excludes a target the attack kills with certainty

Date: 2026-09-19. Issue 117, filed by Chat from a loss breakdown of all three maps at 93c4dec; agreed on the Design Table (tenth round) with Code's amendment to the condition. This changes a rule both partners agreed in the seventh round, so it is recorded here rather than as a code fix.

1. **The hole in the seventh-round rule.** `Exposure.Of` counted an enemy the named attack would have killed, on purpose, as the conservative reading of a worst case. A worst-case veto compares a tile against a threshold and never against the alternative, so when the only path to the win runs through a refused tile the captain chooses a certain loss over an impossible death. On Old Mill Road seed 2 the captain at 14 HP stood two tiles from a boss at 8 HP for twelve turns, declining a kill at raw hit 100 for 9 because the sum counted the boss's counter (15) and enemy-phase strike (15). Every timeout loss on every map under both schemes (197 of 197 at 93c4dec) ended the same way, `bandit_leader-1` alone alive after a long quiet tail.
2. **The rule.** A target the named attack kills with certainty contributes neither its counter nor its enemy-phase term to either sum. Certainty is exact and constant-free: the attacker strikes, its raw hit (`SideForecast.HitChance`, section 5's number after its clamp) is 100, and the first strike's plain damage is at least the target's current HP. One strike, never the double, because the counter falls between the strikes. Everything else in the sum stays as conservative as before: a probable kill still counts in full.
3. **Raw, not displayed.** Chat's proposal named the displayed hit. Under two-roll averaging every raw hit from 95 to 100 prints 100 (raw 96 lands 99.72 percent), and under one roll only raw 100 does, so "displayed 100" is a 99.5 percent threshold in disguise whose width changes with the scheme, the constant the proposal set out to avoid. Raw 100 is `Combat.HitProbability` returning exactly one under either scheme. A captain at raw 96 against a boss is taking a 1-in-357 death for the map, which is a judgment a person makes in a heartbeat and a baseline should not make by rounding; that board stays refused and stalls, and the gate 1 row will say so once #114 prints the quiet tail. Reopen only if the exact rule leaves a map under 60 percent on this account alone.
4. **Measured under the exact condition, 200 seeds, the cast.** Gate 1: Old Mill Road 129/200 to 184/200 under two rolls (p90 8 to 7) and 120 to 164 under one roll, both equal to Chat's numbers under the displayed condition, so no seed had the captain at raw 95 to 99 against the boss; Saltmarsh Ford 171 and 126, the Tollgate 199, all unmoved on gate 1 (the Tollgate's gate 4 still fails the cast, median drop -0.005 under two rolls against -0.003 before, and under one roll names Ottilie and Wren at a median of 0.015, the #115 case), since their stalls end with the boss at full HP where no kill is certain and the refusal is a judgment about risk. Those stalls are content findings for issue 13's second run, not baseline faults. The hit A/B (DECISIONS/0023) stays settled: Saltmarsh Ford, which carried the effect, does not move; Old Mill Road's gap between schemes widens from 9 points to 20 in the same direction.
5. **Falsified both ways** in `ExposureTests`: a certain kill on the yard leaves the other enemy's strike as the whole sum; raw 99 under two rolls prints 100 and still counts both terms; damage one short of the target's HP still counts both; `HeuristicPlayer.PlanUnit` sends a captain the old sum would have refused into the certain kill; and Old Mill Road seed 2 ends in a win at turn 8 instead of the stall. The gate-4 test on the walled fixture now reads damage and absorbed rather than attacks, since the captain takes the certain kill in both arms and the attack counts meet.
6. **Downstream.** Issue 13's second run may now read gate 1 and gate 4 off Old Mill Road (92 percent, median turn 7, p90 7 against a limit of 20; wren's drop 0.855). The eighth round's Old Mill Road line was mostly this instrument fault, recorded in the tenth round.
