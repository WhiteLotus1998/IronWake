## What is built

- **The pack.** `CampaignRecord.Kitted` puts every heirloom bound to a unit behind their cast pack at full uses when the campaign first fields them: at the start, at `StartAt`, and for a map's arrivals. Teodor's campaign pack is Iron Lance, Field Dressing, Family Lance, so the iron is equipped and the lance is swung only by choice, and `item teodor 2` stays the dressing in every journaled script. `units/cast.json` is unchanged, so no sample or `play` board carries the lance. A first cut put it in the cast file, and that broke about twenty journaled sample transcripts for a campaign rule.
- **The gate.** A ladder's optional `held` (a card line) holds its last stage on any stack whose `GateOpen` is false (`Heirloom.Held`). The counter runs on. The gate holds everywhere, including samples and side maps, since only a won quest opens it. The card appends the line (`Placeholder. The rust holds; it waits on Teodor.`) once the count has passed the held stage, so the stop does not read as a bug (pillar 2, round 267). The stack's `gateOpen` rides the protocol and the save.
- **The waking.** A part 1 quest's `wakes` names a gated heirloom bound to its member. The loader refuses it on a part 2 quest, on a weapon with no `held`, or on another member's weapon. A win opens the gate (`Heirloom.OpenGate`), and the lance wakes at once when it is at sound with the count at 10 or more: `teodor wins teodor_1; Family Lance wakes in Teodor's hands`. Otherwise the line is `will wake in Teodor's hands`, and the next combat that reaches 10 turns it as usual. A lost or skipped quest leaves the gate shut, so without the quest the lance never wakes, and the reseal's lance clause can fail. Every shipped gated heirloom has a quest that wakes it (a test, not a loader rule, so fixture campaigns without quests still load).
- **The board.** `content/quests/the_old_watch.map`: defeat boss, 14x9, limit 9, Recall 2, enemy level 2, `announce: on`. The Sworn Captain (the boss) holds the cairn's fort at 7,0, with a held archer at 7,1 and a held soldier in the one-tile door at 7,2. A water band on rows 4 and 5 has one crossing near the cairn, the bridge at 7,4 and 7,5. Two hill brigands sleep at 3,2 and 11,2. An announced rear pair comes up the hill: a brigand at 0,8 on turn 3 and a soldier at 13,8 on turn 4. The twist is the bridgehead. Only one enemy can stand on 7,3 to strike 7,4, and the guard boss sallies off his fort to take that tile against a unit he judges weak, then goes home to heal. Long Thrust is the only reach over the door.
- `campaign.json`'s `teodor_1`: part 1, `opensAfter: harrow_weir` (Teodor's arrival is not named yet, the same hold as Pell's, 0199), `wakes: family_lance`, no door and no material. Appended at the end of `quests`, so no other side map's seed moves (0198). The cards are placeholders with the rules line.
- `--heirloom <item> --quest` adds the quest arm: at the first camp that offers the waking quest, the run plays it with the owner and the first other non-captain on the roster, retried like a map, and passes it by when no try wins. The run's forced issue now moves the carried stack to the front instead of adding a second one.
