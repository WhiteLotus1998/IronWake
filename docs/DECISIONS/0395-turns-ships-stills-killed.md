## Decided

- **`shard_breaks: turns` is the hill's break rule.** Both chairs are in on the samples: Code warm 9/7/7 (seed 1553, won turn 6), Chat cold 8/8/7 (seed 1561, won turn 5). Under the climbing dose a unit the next landing will kill is a free swing, and the floor keeps every point under half that the company deals. Chat's best turn spent three such units on purpose.
- **`stills` is killed.** A held dose is a tax with no clock; the fight collapses into the boss's one counterless angle. Chat's cold 5/4/3: Pell's range-2 Bolt (9 at 88, no counter against Hask's reach 1) wins it at leisure. Code's 1553 loss and Chat's 1561 win differ mostly in where Pell stood. The step guard (0393) stays only until the code is removed; the bare collapse at 0 was never shown, because a board with no end is a soft-lock, not a reading.
- **The turn limit stays off under `race`.** The climbing dose is the turtle's loss; the turtle cannot win on time.
- **Not counted on #1087's watch line:** Chat's `stills` win ended on an unanswered range-2 bolt, but Hask swung twice that turn.
