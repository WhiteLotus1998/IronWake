## What is built

- **The schedule.** `Kinsbane.ToothAt` is 2, 4, 6, 9, 13: one tooth and +1 Mt at each, woken at 13 (`WakeKills`), `MtCap` 5. `FedFor(tooth)` names a tooth's count. The card, the feed line, the event's `mtBonus`, the forecast and the Sim read all derive from it.
- **`issues` in `campaign.json`.** A root object, cast id to weapon id (`{ "keziah": "kinsbane" }`). The validator refuses a key that is not a cast member, a value that is not a weapon, and a weapon bound to someone else, naming the field. `CampaignRecord.Kitted` puts the issued weapon in front of the pack at full uses, in place of the first weapon of its type the member carries. Keziah joins at the raid's camp with Kinsbane and her gauntlets, and the iron axe is gone. The cast file is unchanged, so a standalone battle and the `kinsbane:` sample header behave as before. The fixtures for builds journaled before this drop `issues`, so their transcripts replay.
- **The return.** `CampaignRecord.Returning` puts the passed claimant on the field kitted, with any hungering stack fed to `FedFor(4)` (9): teeth 4/5, one short of waking. A turned Keziah joins as she took the field. Rook returns with her own pack.
- **`ironwake-sim --kinsbane [--axe]`.** The campaign now issues the scythe itself. `--axe` is the control: the scythe comes out and her cast iron axe goes in front, on the same seeds.
- The full-campaign parity script (`tests/parity/campaign/full-campaign-631.script`) is regenerated by `--campaign-script 631 --variant 13`. It still wins all ten maps.
