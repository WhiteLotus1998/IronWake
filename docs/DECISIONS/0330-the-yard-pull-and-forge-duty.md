# 0330: The yard's pull, practice weapons, and what forge duty buys

Date: 2026-10-08. Design Table #1334, rounds 447 (Code) and 448 (Chat). Answers 0329's Unsure.

## Decided

- **The teacher's blows pull, always on.** In the yard, a hit from the teacher that would kill a drill hand leaves it at 1 HP instead. This covers counters and crits. It is not a flag the player can switch; the Sim may keep it as an ablation arm. The forecast prints it, because rules are on screen. A pulled hand still swings in its own phase, so softening every hand does not make the drill free. Without the pull the teacher's only verb is the kill, which contradicts "the teacher earns nothing, the student needs the kills". With it, the teacher's turn is the choice: soften or screen.
- **Drill hands carry a practice weapon.** It is a placeholder `drill` weapon, with an original name later, low Mt and no crit. It is tuned so that at equal level a lone hand needs three hits to kill the student. This fixes what the hand plays found (the student died to two hits and needed three). Two hands on the student is then the danger, readable from turn 1.
- **Order for #1332:** the hands' numbers first, then the pull, then the pen. The pen gives the choice its shape but cannot carry the drill alone.
- **Wounded: refused the yard only.** Side maps stay open to a wounded unit, as shipped, so a fall never quietly closes a story door. The forge opens to the wounded, reversing 0329's refusal.
- **Forge duty buys one refine step with no gold** (#1344). The step goes on a weapon the unit carries, the material is still paid, and every existing refusal applies. Plain `refine` is unchanged. Forge duty is refused until the forge is built. The lever, if the duty is never worth a body: waive the material too.
