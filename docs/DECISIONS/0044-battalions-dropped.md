# 0044 — Battalions are dropped from v1; forks are for matters outside the game

## Context
DESIGN section 12 kept battalions in Phase 3 with their shape open, and the Table's sixth round said the decision PR of the first Cha spike would file the fork. Issue 16's keep PR (DECISIONS/0043) filed it as #225, both partners leaning drop.

## Lotus's answer
He declined to rule: "that's not a fork for me. That's all of your decision." His opinion, offered and not a ruling: battalions are cool but a little overcomplicated for a first iteration, and the partners could make up a mechanic of their own instead of porting one.

## Decision
Dropped from v1. Cha keeps its two jobs: the captain's Cha scales Commander's Word (13.2, #85) and a recruit's Cha sets the rate rapport accrues (13.1, #16). Adjacency already means something through rivalry, and a second formation system would compete with it for the same tiles. The between-map screen (#74) is unblocked and its campaign record carries no battalion slot.

Lotus's second thought is taken as a standing invitation, not an item: if the campaign ever wants a formation layer, it comes back as a section 13 experiment of our own design, played before it is specced, and not as a port of gambits.

## Process change
Lotus sent this fork back, so the `fork` label narrows. It now covers only what lies outside the game: money, the repo's visibility, external services and accounts, licenses, and anything Lotus has asked to be asked about. Adding or dropping a system, changing section 2's architecture, changing a pillar, or rewriting content is a Table decision with a decision record. CLAUDE.md's "Decide vs. escalate" says so.

## Consequences
- DESIGN section 12: battalions removed, this record cited.
- #225 closed; #74 loses `blocked`.
- The Builder's housekeeping step for fork rulings is unchanged; it will simply see fewer of them.
