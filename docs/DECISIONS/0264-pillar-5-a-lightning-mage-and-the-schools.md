# 0264: Pillar 5 takes an eleventh recruit, a lightning mage; the magic schools mapped

Date: 2026-10-06. Lotus's rulings relayed on Design Table #1112 (6005914577, the five schools; 6005965629, the lightning mage; 6006017776, learning schools). Chat 6005978542 and 6006098166, Code's reply in round 383. Nothing here is built; the spell lists wait on Lotus's spell pass.

## Context

Lotus gave each magic school an identity: fire burns over turns, ice/water slows, earth buffs and supports, lightning hits hardest and has an end-game spell that can stun; Light (healing) and the dark grimoire stand. With five schools and Pell the only attack mage in the cast (only Pell, Mag 6, and Maud, Mag 4, start with Magic), most schools would never be seen. So Lotus added a character: a lightning mage, a man, joining somewhere along the way.

## Decision

- **Pillar 5 (Lotus's ruling):** 11 recruits plus the captain. DESIGN section 1 and section 14 are amended. Where he joins, his region and his voice are open to the Fable story pass and Lotus's character pass. The partners' direction is Pell's opposite (he spends, she keeps), from Kestrow, joining around maps 5 to 7.
- **Ice/water slows (Lotus).** It strikes the lean "Water never chills" (round 366). An ice tome's hit uses frozen iron's chill (0155). Rook's frost hold wins over it and the two never stack.
- **The schools are tags on Lore tomes** (provisional, round 383). Fire is Cinder. Lightning is Bolt. Ice and earth have no tome yet. Faith is Light. Dark is the drain grimoire. Pell's Commonplace stays unschooled. Gust is open until Lotus's spell pass: Chat places it in lightning as storm, and Code would leave it unschooled, because a Hit 100 tome contradicts lightning's price of accuracy and uses.
- **The riders are provisional leans:**
  - Fire's burn is a flat 2 HP at the start of each of the target's side's next two phases, never below 1. A second burn refreshes it and never stacks. It reuses `burning` and `unitBurned`.
  - Lightning's end-game stun is native to the lightning mage only. A stunned unit skips its next phase, and bosses are spared.
  - Earth never heals. Its first shape is open: Chat leans toward a ward (+Def until the ally's next phase ends). Code leans toward raised ground, a fort-grade tile under the ally until the caster's next phase, because a defence that comes from a tile keeps round 155's rule.
- **The Marshal and Commander reach fire and lightning. The Adept reaches fire, ice and lightning. The Scholar adds earth** (a lean).
- **Learning schools from tomes and doors goes to Lotus.** The partners' recommendation is on #1112 (Chat 6006098166, Code round 383).

## Consequences

- The company cap (12 living) and the beds (the cast plus one spare) were sized for a cast of ten and the captain. The build that seats him owes a read of both, so that his arrival doesn't silently close the barracks and Bet's hire.
