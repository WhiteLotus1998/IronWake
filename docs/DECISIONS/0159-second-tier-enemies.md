# 0159 — The second tier's enemies and its timing (#704, slice 3)

Date: 2026-10-02. Issue #704 (Lotus's progression batch, item 1; Chat's round 216; slices 1 and 2 are 0157 and 0158). The shape is the Table's; the choices below are Code's. Provisional.

## Decided

- **One template per form**, each its base template's level, stats and growths in the form, so what changes on the board is the form's modifiers and verb: Veteran (Halberdier; Steel Lance, Iron Axe), Marauder (Berserker; Steel Axe), Longbowman (Marksman; Steel Bow, 2-3), Lector (Scholar; Bolt, Radiance), Deacon (Warden; Radiance, Salve, Iron Sword), Heavy Rider (Lancer; Steel Lance, Iron Axe), Wing Captain (Sky Captain; Steel Lance), Sentry (Sentinel; Steel Lance, Iron Bow). Each carries steel or a D spell and declares rank D in it. They scale to a map's `enemy_level` like any template. Plain English names under #701's rule.
- **Dusk's unseen reach is now 9** (Wing Captain: Mov 7 plus range 2), as 0158 said it would be once a Sky Captain template shipped: the reach reads the classes the content's templates are in, not the board. Brackwater Cut's journaled dusk plays (283, and the exit sample's 53) replay on a fixture without the second tier, the record of the build they were played on.
- **No shipped map fields them yet.** The issue's acceptance measures gate 1 with them both with and without the player's tier. Without it, on `docs/samples/sallow_grange_advanced.map` (four of the seven swapped for their forms), gate 1 falls from 157/200 to 106/200 (53 percent, failed). The player's tier is not reachable on today's campaign (below), so the "with" arm cannot be had, and fielding them now would only flatten the last third the other way. `docs/measurements/sallow_grange_advanced-704.txt`.
- **The timing measure.** `--levels [--seeds N]` in the Sim fights the whole campaign under the heuristic player (permadeath off, standing in for the Recall a player spends on a death; a map retried on fresh seeds as `--heirloom` does) and prints, per map, how many of the company are at level 10 or above, how many meet some form's level, ranks and stats as if in its base (the heuristic never certifies), the highest and third-highest levels, and the levels the company has gained. On 200 seeds (`docs/measurements/levels-704.txt`): at map 7 nobody is at 10 and nobody meets a form; the highest unit is level 6 (p50) and the third highest 2; the company has gained 7 levels in all. 118 of 200 runs never win map 1 (Starting Alone's known 1/200).

## Open

- The issue's rule reads "if the median puts nobody there, the level moves down." On this measure it would move to about 5, which is the first step's level (3 or 4) plus one, and rank C is out of reach besides. The heuristic is a thin proxy for EXP: it feeds the captain and gains the company a level a map. Lean, for the Table: hold level 10 and rank C until a campaign play from a chair reports the company's levels at map 7, then set the gate from that, and field the templates on maps 7 to 10 in the same PR.
