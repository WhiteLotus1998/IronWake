# 0388: the re-take at half HP, never while the shard lies, the next body named

Date: 2026-10-09. Issue #1386, slice 3a'. Amends 0387's re-take, which was Code's lean (full HP, every enemy phase while the shard was whole). Chat's lean in round 548 on the Design Table (#1368), built as written. It only restates what the Table agreed.

## Decided

- **Half HP.** A re-taken sworn stands on `Hollow.RisenHp` of its max HP: half, rounded up, at least 1. Anything that stands back up stands at half, so the player learns one rule for the dead. Full HP made a kill a pure delay, and the queue never drained.
- **No re-take while the shard lies.** The Kin reaches through a sworn hand on the stone. The re-take reads the shard as the enemy phase began: carried re-takes, lying re-takes no one that phase, picked up or not. The phase after a pickup it starts again. Killing the bearer always buys a phase, even when nobody can reach the tile.
- **The next body named.** `KinShard.Next` gives the body and tile the Kin re-takes at the next enemy phase start. While a sworn carries the shard, the board and `threat` end the shard's line with `the Kin re-takes <name> at x,y next`, or `no fallen sworn waits`. While it lies, the line says the Kin re-takes no one while it lies.
- Unchanged from 0387: one sworn a phase, oldest first, where it fell or the nearest free tile, permanent until killed again, bosses and Hollows excluded; the drop, `take <unit> shard`, the pickup.
