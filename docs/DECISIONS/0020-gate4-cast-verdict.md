# 0020 — Gate 4's map-level verdict and the same-set mixes

Date: 2026-09-19. Issue 105, filed by Chat from a `--full` run on the Tollgate at 14cedea. Agreed on PR #104 and the Design Table (eighth round); Builder's shape for the guard.

1. **A median drop at or below zero fails the cast, not a recruit.** Gate 4's relative threshold (`drop + 2 SE < 0.5 * median`) still fires with a negative median, since half a negative number is a lower negative number, and on the Tollgate it named the two recruits who cost the party most and cleared the one who cost it least, on a scale whose zero point was below zero. `Gates.CastFails` reads the median first: at or below zero the gate fails with its own line ("cast not earning its deployment: benching the median recruit raises the win rate by X; rows are data, no recruit is judged"), the rows print without a label, and `Judge` returns nobody. The guard is falsified by a constructed Escape map where the second recruit starts nine tiles from the exit under a two-turn limit, so the party wins only with that recruit benched (drop -1.000 on that recruit), and by a row test with the Tollgate's own numbers. Above zero the rule is unchanged and the walled-off test stays green.
2. **The verdict belongs to gate 4, not issue 47.** Issue 47's dead-turn count says where the idleness is; gate 4 is the gate that says the deployment is wrong. A gate that hands its verdict to a diagnostic stops being a gate. The negative median is what the seventh round's authoring rule (about one contested place per two deployed units, or deploy fewer) looks like when the Sim measures its violation, and section 11's premise "a body always matters" is dropped as falsified by the run.
3. **Three mixes per row, the same set on both sides of the bench.** The old row compared one recruit's baseline against every unit still deployed in the arm summed, captain included, which on the Tollgate was one against four with the captain's share printed nowhere. `AblationRow` now carries the recruit's own baseline mix, the rest of the cast's baseline mix, and the rest of the cast's benched mix, so a small drop beside a rest-of-cast that absorbed a third more is the healer case section 11 was written for, read off the row.
4. **The captain's mix prints, unjudged.** A last row `captain <id>: baseline [...] never benched, not judged`. Chat's prediction is that the veto makes the captain a piece to position rather than swing on maps 5 to 8; his attack count on those maps confirms or refutes it from the instrument rather than from either partner.
5. **Not built, by the same round:** the heuristic gets no healing-terrain preference (the lone captain on Old Mill Road refuses the boss at full HP, 32 against a ceiling of 22, so a fort would move where he waits and change nothing; the Def and Res term inside the exposure sum is the terrain sense the baseline is allowed), and recruits take no veto (the captain's refusal is a rule because his death is the loss; a recruit's would be a policy, and a baseline with policies measures itself instead of the map, the exact confound gate 4 exists to avoid). Old Mill Road's 40 percent is a content finding for issue 13: two cadets kill the boss in one phase and one cannot kill it at all, so the map ends when the second body dies and admits it fourteen turns later.
