## Decided

- **The finisher** (`Players.cs`, `Finisher`): a unit declares an art it knows with a per-map charge (the captain's Full Measure) when that art, a single strike, kills on its non-crit first hit a **swallowed** boss whose fall ends the map (`EndsTheMap`: Defeat Boss, the only boss standing, every boss spawn fired, no swallow left; a first bar that swallows never qualifies). It is weighed ahead of every strike, cast and shell. A landed hit ends the map, so only the miss is priced: a unit the veto covers takes a tile when the hit is at least 90 (`FinisherHit`) or it survives the miss there (`MissPrice`): for an art that costs the next phase, the veto's no-crit sum twice, the second phase's Frozen Iron landing in place of the first's. Best hit, then the lower price, then row order.
- **Clear** (`Clear`): with a swallowed boss standing whose fall ends the map, a captain with no finisher and no strike the veto allows, whose walk or stand would end where that boss's lance or line strike reaches (`InBossReach`), ends instead on the tile in his reach the boss does not reach and whose no-crit sum is under his HP, nearest the walk's tile (then the lower sum, then row order). With none, he walks as before. Frozen Iron lands on every unit, so there is no tile clear of the dose (round 537).
- **90 stays.** The 80 screen plays identically on the Warden sample (127 / 107 / 0): every lethal offer is at 90 or above, median 99 %.
- **Stage 2 alone, for now.** Rounds 535 and 536 read the finisher on any boss whose fall ends the map, and 535 expected the other maps to keep their cells. They do not: the captain knows Full Measure on every map, and on every Defeat Boss boss the finisher moved two tuned maps, Harrow Weir 132 to 164 and the field 103 to 133 of 200 (every other cell unchanged), and the campaign keep, whose `hask` carries no stage, 149 / 135 / 0 to 160 / 143 / 1. A tuned map moving 10 or more goes to the Table, so the finisher ships on a swallowed boss only. Only `hask_warden` carries a swallow, so no cell outside the Warden sample can move: Harrow Weir 132 and the keep 149 / 135 / 0, `finale: ok`, checked on the scoped build.
- **On the Warden sample** (`docs/measurements/keep-1441-plan.txt`): 93 / 91 / 0 to **127 / 107 / 0**; full passes gate 1's 120, depleted does not. Full's stage-2 captain struck Hask in 117 phases (106 with the art; 11 before), ended a phase in his reach 39 times (198), and lost 37 in the stage (71); no lost game had a finisher left on offer.
