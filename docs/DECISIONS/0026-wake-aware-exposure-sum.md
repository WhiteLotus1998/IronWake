# 0026 — The exposure sum counts the board the command certainly produces, wake included

Date: 2026-09-24. Issue 128, filed by Chat from a scratch sweep of all three maps at ff8e03f; the rule agreed on the Design Table's twelfth round and its wording on the thirteenth (DIALOGUE.md). This changes a rule both partners agreed in the seventh round, so it is recorded here rather than as a code fix.

1. **The hole in the seventh-round rule.** `Exposure.Of` priced a sleeping Guard group from where it stood, on purpose, as the conservative reading of a worst case. It built its board as the state with the mover moved and never ran section 8's wake check, so a destination within the wake radius of a sleeping group was priced with that group at Hold while the move itself guaranteed the group awake and moving on the enemy phase that followed. On Old Mill Road, gate 4's captain-alone arm under two rolls, 167 of 200 games ended in a captain death, every one dealt by `soldier-1` of the sleeping `mill` group with a `GroupWoke` before it: the veto existed to stop exactly this and priced the one enemy that killed him at zero.
2. **The rule (twelfth round).** The sum counts the board the enemy phase will find as far as this command certainly determines it: an enemy that is certainly dead is not on it (DECISIONS/0024), a group this command certainly wakes is awake on it, and a strike that certainly misses is not thrown on it (issue 126). Certainly woken is section 8's three causes, taken from one predicate the resolver reads too, `WakeCheck.Run`, so the veto and the rules cannot drift apart: proximity to where the player's units stand after the move, the mover included; noise from the fight's two tiles when the plan attacks; and the certain death of a member, the target's own group at any distance. A group the command does not wake stays counted from where it stands, which is still the conservative reading in that regime. All three causes in the first pass, death included: no current map can fire death alone, since every group that can sleep spreads at most 5 against a noise radius of 6 and no class moves past 6, but it is the rule that stays true once a map spreads a group, and leaving it out would be a special case.
3. **The word is certainty, not futurity (thirteenth round, Chat's amendment).** The veto is a worst case over the board at the moment of the command, moved only by what the command certainly determines, and is described as a guarantee over the cycle nowhere. A recruit's later command that wakes a group is not this command's certainty (the ordering hole, measured harmless and closed). An ally's body in a one-tile crossing that dies inside the cycle and opens it is the enemy's choice, never this command's certainty, so the sum keeps pricing the enemy behind it at zero (DECISIONS/0025); the test `AnAllyInAOneTileCrossingBlocksTheEnemyBehindItInTheSum` asserts that as deliberate, and gate 1's captain-loss count is where that regime is seen.
4. **Built.** `Exposure.Board` is the board the sum is priced on: the mover on the tile, a certainly killed target removed (its tile is then free for the group it woke), the fight's two tiles noisy, every group `WakeCheck` says the command wakes woken. `Resolver.WakeGroups` reads the dead and the fought tiles off the command's events and calls the same predicate. Falsified in `ExposureTests`: a tile at exactly the wake radius counts the group by its move and a tile one past it with no fight counts nothing, though the awake group would reach it; the target's tile alone wakes a group the destination does not; a certain kill on a grouped enemy wakes a member outside both radii by death and the same board with no death listed wakes nothing; the sleeping-guard test of the seventh round, whose own fixture woke the group on turn 1, is replaced.
5. **Measured, 200 seeds, both schemes,** in issue 128's PR against the rows of #137. Gate 1 does not move by a game on any map; the whole effect lands in gate 4's benched arms, which is where issue 13's second run reads its numbers.
6. **Reopen only** if a map spreads a group past the noise radius and a hand play finds the death cause pricing a group the player could not have known would wake; then the fix is the map, since the rule is section 8's own.
