# 0217 — Rook's Drover replaces the Scout

Date: 2026-10-03. Issue #872. Supersedes 0167. The class comes from Lotus's change relayed on the Table (#820, round 288). Chat pinned the details in rounds 289 and 290; the pins are copied on #872. The shapes below are the Builder's and are provisional.

## Decided

- **The class** (`drover`, name Drover): `advances: skyrider`, `unique: rook`, `unlockedBy: rook_1`, flying, Mov 7, lance, the Sky Captain's certification, seal, modifiers (Spd +3, Res +2, Str +1) and growths, and mastery Lookout. The double-for-bite trade is the whole difference.
- **Never doubling** is a class field, `singleStrike: true` (`UnitClass.SingleStrike`). `Combat.Doubles` reads it from `Combatant.Class`, so it holds both when she strikes and when she counters (round 289). A unique class has to name what it loses, so the new `SidegradeMeasure.Doubling` reads 1 strike per combat for the Drover and 2 for the Sky Captain.
- **Drake Bite** is a `bite` effect (`halfGrown` 3, `grown` 5), read off the rider's `Unit.Drake`; with no drake there is no bite. `SideForecast.Bite` is non-zero only at distance 1. The resolver applies the bite once, after the exchange, when one of that side's strikes hit and both units still stand. It is the attacker's bite, or else the defender's; one bite per combat. The bite never rolls and is not a `StrikeEvent`. That means EXP per strike, ranks, masteries, rapport, chill, Opening, grounding and wildfire never see it, and Brace's -15 cannot touch it. `CombatResult.Bite` and `CombatFought.Bite` carry it, and both sides' HP after the combat include it, so a bite that kills is that combat's kill.
- **Where the bite shows.** The lethal readings add it: `AttackerDamageLivedFor` adds it when the counter, every strike landing, leaves her standing, and `CounterIfAllLand` (and so `CounterIsLethal`) adds it to her counter. The forecast line prints `x1, never doubles,` and `; drake bites N if a strike hits and both stand, no roll`. The log prints `The drake bites: N (<target> hp M)`. The protocol adds `bite` and `neverDoubles` to a forecast side and `bite` to `combatFought`.
- **Long Carry** (`long_carry`, from Grown): after a carry (0215), a rider holding it keeps a Canto on the Move the flight left, read from her reach with the ally lifted.
- **Deep Rime** (`deep_rime`, `rounds` 1, at Unbroken): every tile her breath freezes carries `RimeTile.Extra`. A tile that comes due with a round left goes back to clock 1 instead of thawing, so it holds one more enemy phase and one more player phase. The board line says `then holds 1 more round (deep rime)`, and the protocol's rime tile carries `extra`.
- **On screen:** `classes` prints `the drake: Drake Bite (half-grown 3, grown 5; never doubles, live); Long Carry (from grown, ...); Deep Rime (at unbroken, ...)` under the class, with live or not yet for the unit asked about. Rook's card adds `Live: Drake Bite 5, Long Carry` at her stage.
- **Gone with the Scout:** High Watch and Headcount, the `sight` and `headcount` effect kinds, the `OnSight` trigger, `BattleUnit.ExtraSight`, `Dusk.Line`'s names argument, `SleepingThreat.CountedBy`/`Priced`, and the protocol's `countedBy`, `priced` and `ifWokenAllLand`. The art files are renamed `scout_*` to `drover_*`; they are byte-identical, still drawn as the Skyrider.
- **The full-campaign parity script** moves from variant 13 to 14. The class list a certify tries is rotated by the variant and sorted by id, and `drover` sorts where `scout` did not. Variant 14 rebuilds the committed script line for line, apart from its header.
- **The measure** is `ironwake-sim --drover` (`docs/measurements/drover-872.txt`). It puts Rook at levels 7, 10 and 15, Half-grown and Grown, against all 30 enemy templates raised to her level on plain, and splits the player phase from the enemy phase.

## The read, and what it does not move

The bar was that she trails on at least a third of the templates and leads on at least a third. It **fails** at every level and stage. At Grown she is behind on 24 of 30 and ahead on 6 (level 10: 25 and 5), and the enemy phase reads the same. She leads exactly where the Sky Captain cannot double: the sentry, both shieldbearers, the finale's lord and hunter at some levels, the wingrider, and the wing captain at 15. Everywhere else, a second strike of 9 to 12 is worth more than a bite of 5. Neither of the issue's levers fires: she does not lead everywhere (cut to 4) and does not trail everywhere (+1). One +1 would not close a gap of a whole strike anyway. The numbers ship as the Table agreed them, and the read goes to the Table (round 292).
