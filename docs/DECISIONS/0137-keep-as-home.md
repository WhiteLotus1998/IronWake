# 0137 — 13.20 The keep as a home: beds, rooms and the kill criterion

Date: 2026-10-01. Issue 536, agreed on the Design Table in rounds 206 (Chat) and 207 (Code) on #665. This record restates what the Table agreed; it decides nothing new. Provisional, like every experiment.

## Why

0051's purse ended a campaign at 1120 with nothing to buy (seed 139). Rooms give the purse a want before the raid, and since they spend the same purse as the finale's walls, a bed is a wall the keep does not have. 13.5 folds into this instead of standing beside it.

## Decided

- **Beds.** The keep starts with beds for the captain and the five, plus one spare (seven). Every member who has joined holds a bed, living or fallen; a death never frees one (0010: no rule may make losing a recruit pay).
- **No bed, no join.** A meeting with no free bed prints `no bed free: <name> will not join` at the meeting. There is no benching. The branch's return on map 7 (section 14) reads the same rule.
- **Rooms in v1.** The bunk room, +1 bed, 400 (the price of a wall), at most two. The forge (#647), 600, opened once the smith is met. Nothing else: Supports (#77) are lines on the roster, not a room, which supersedes #536's original "companions are talked to in the keep".
- **When.** Rooms can be bought at every camp from map 1. Walls and ditches come after the raid (0060). The purse carries over.
- **The sum.** Today's finale menu is two walls at 400 and two ditches at 300 (1400); the rooms are two bunks and the forge (1400). The kill clause reads "every room and every wall", not this number, so a price change does not move it.
- **Pillar 5 is unchanged.** The pool is the ten recruits we have (0121); the seeded draw of new recruits and the pillar amendment #536 first asked for are dropped.

## Keep test

- **Kept** if in either chair's campaign play a room is bought or refused with the finale's walls named in the journal.
- **Killed** if the purse at the raid screen affords every room and every wall in both plays. Beds then become a fixed count granted by the story.
