## The rules as built

- `campaign.json` `supports`: `{a, b, kind}`, two different cast members, each pair listed once. The kinds are argument, comedy, debt, mentor, respect (a rivalry turned respect) and romance.
- Every recruit has exactly one pair with the captain and three or four with other recruits.
- No two of a recruit's pairs share a kind.
- **The captain is exempt from the kind rule.** Ten pairs can't fit six kinds, so the amendment's "no two of one character's pairs" can't hold for the captain. This is the one reading the amendment allowed.
- **Orientation:** a captain pair may carry `romance: [pronouns]`. With a captain of one of those pronouns, the pair is a romance instead of its kind, and it counts against the recruit's other romance. A captain pair's own kind is never romance.
- At least one romance is between two recruits who share a pronoun.

## Why these

- **Romance stays with the claimants, evenly.** Rook and Keziah are each open to either captain, so the branch choice never doubles as a romance choice. That answers part of Lotus's worry, relayed on #780 when the story gate opened, that Keziah has to be as magnetic as the drake.
- **Pell and Maud** carry round 192's same-sex pair and are written as people. Brannock is the gay man among the captain's options.
- Each captain gender gets three romance options.
