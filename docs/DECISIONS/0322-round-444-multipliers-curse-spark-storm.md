# 0322: Multipliers multiply, Curse stands beside burn, Spark Storm drops the flier rider

Date: 2026-10-07. Rounds 443 and 444 on the Design Table (#1287, #1334). Settles the two items Lotus's round-3 spell rulings left to the Table.

## Decided

- **Status and spell damage multipliers multiply** and apply to final damage after Def or Res, rounded down once at the end. Spark Storm's mark (x1.5) and Lightning Rod's charge (x1.25) give x1.875 together; a marked holder catching a spell takes x0.75. Effectiveness is not one of these: it stays a multiplier on Mt (DESIGN 5).
- **Lightning Rod's charge** is spent on his next lightning cast, hit or miss, does not stack, and ends with the map. The rod catches single-target casts only.
- **Curse** is its own status, one a unit, beside burn and outside burn's cap of 4. A recast refreshes it for the new caster. Blind is -30 Hit. The tick floors at 1 HP and heals a living caster by what it took; a dead caster's curse still ticks and heals nobody. Light's cleanse clears it.
- **Spark Storm** drops Gust's fliers-effective rider. An area cast hits enemies only and draws no counter.
- **For #1247's revision:** the meteor (range 3 to 5, lands the caster's next phase, half on the ring, two burn stacks, friendly fire kept, the enemy planner prices marked tiles); the fault (four tiles, Sunder closes it early); the ice top as a whiteout (enemies within 1 chilled, the already chilled frozen), with a plain blizzard as fallback; the Ledger class's price listed both ways (staves at half, or light passing over the bearer).

## Why

One multiplier rule covers every future one; the difference from adding is small and not worth a special case. Curse as a debt beside burn keeps burn's cap meaningful and gives light a job against dark. Spark Storm is the setup; the lightning that cashes it does the killing.
