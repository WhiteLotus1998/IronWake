## The clause, refined (round 111)

Round 110 wrote the clause as a win rate over the games that did not stall (Harrow Weir 116 of 150, 77 percent). Chat's round 111 showed that this is the exclusion gate 1 itself rejects for protected deaths, and that it is loose: a map at 80 wins, 70 timeouts and 50 captain deaths is 40 percent counted and 61.5 percent over the rest, and would have passed while the refusal sat near one. It also showed that the median proves only half the timeouts are stalls, not all of them.
