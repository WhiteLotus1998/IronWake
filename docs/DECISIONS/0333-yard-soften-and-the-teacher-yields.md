# 0333: The Sim's teacher softens; a teacher who falls in the yard yields

Date: 2026-10-08. Design Table #1334, rounds 450 and 451. Follows 0329, 0331 and 0332.

## Decided

- **The yard arm gets no verdict until #1332's practice weapon is in.** The first read (0332) measured full-strength hands on the placeholder board.
- **Teacher falls are the first number read on the rerun, with a target of near zero.** The first read had 64 teacher falls in 87 drills with the pull and 35 in 103 without it, so the hands hit an L6 teacher too hard either way and the pull, which keeps hands alive around him, nearly doubles it. The practice weapon's brief covers the teacher: one five levels up should barely be scratched.
- **The Sim's camp learns to soften, in #1332, beside the practice weapon.** The rule carries no number: the teacher strikes a hand only when the student can reach and finish it this phase, read off the forecast the planner already has; otherwise he screens. A rule with nothing tuned in it cannot be tuned to the wrong board, and without it every rerun pits a teacher who never uses the pull against one who cannot, so the no-pull arm wins by construction. The arm without softening stays available as an ablation.
- **A teacher who falls in the yard yields.** The drill is lost and both duties are spent, as on any lost drill, but the teacher takes no wound and no death, whatever the record's permadeath. A training run never maims the best unit in the company. The student's fall keeps the shipped rule (permadeath as the record holds it): the student's risk is the lesson, the teacher's is not.

## Unsure

- Whether the drill should play on with the student alone after the teacher yields. Lean no: ending it is simpler and protects a student who would face the crowd alone under permadeath.
