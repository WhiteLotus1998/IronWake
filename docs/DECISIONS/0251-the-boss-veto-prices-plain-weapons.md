# 0251 — The boss veto prices plain weapons only; baiting it with a saved art is skill

Date: 2026-10-05. Round 372 (#1063); closes #1067. Source play: Code's warm Harrow Weir replay under 0237, seed 1360 (`docs/transcripts/2026-10-05-harrow_weir-1360.txt`).

## Context

On Harrow Weir 1360 a noise fight at 9,7 woke the weir, and the Foreman, with no strike in reach, approached under 0080's clause. `Exposure.OfBoss` priced 12,10 at 19 against his 28 on plain weapons, so the veto let him strike Teodor there. Turn 9 put 33 into him: Dunstan 9, Teodor's Long Thrust at range 2 from his own tile, and the captain's Full Measure for 17 at 100. The veto counts no arts, as DESIGN 8 writes it.

## Decision

1. **The veto stays as written: plain weapons, one striker per seat.** A boss that priced each unit's unspent art would read the player's hand, and a once-a-map art would become a deterrent nobody fires. The veto is readable because it counts exactly what `threat` prints; a player who saves what it cannot see is playing it as DESIGN 8 intends ("a rule on the screen a player can bait").
2. **Long Thrust stays out on the same principle.** `OfBoss` already seats range-2 strikers on any plain weapon whose range reaches (a bow, a javelin); a range an art adds is declared and unspent, so it is not counted, the same as Full Measure's numbers.
3. **0170 is not broken by this play.** "Full Measure opens a boss fight but does not end it" is about the boss on his own ground, with terrain and seats on his side. A boss baited onto open plain is a different fight, and a finisher ending it there is the bait's payoff.
4. **Nothing is built.** Gate 1 and every Defeat Boss map are untouched; no `--full` re-run is owed for a rule that does not change.

## Tripwire

If the next two Harrow Weir plays, by any chair, warm or cold, both win by baiting the Foreman off the hill, #1067 reopens: the bait would be dominant rather than a choice. The lever then is content on this map (the Foreman's guard radius or his approach clause), never the veto rule.

## Cost

Harrow now has two routes to its boss: 0100's crest puzzle and the bait. The second is earned only through a wake that pulls him (0080), so a player who never makes that noise still meets the tuned puzzle.
