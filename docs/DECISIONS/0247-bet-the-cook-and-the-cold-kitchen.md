- **Bet was the old garrison's cook for twenty years, not its gate sergeant.** Lotus flagged the gate sergeant and "Twenty years on a town gate. Has opinions about gates." as a near-miss of a well-known character from another franchise. She found the foundling against the outside of the bake-oven wall and handed them to Hask.
