Date: 2026-10-05. Round 380 (Design Table #1112; Code 6001960218, Chat 6003379706), Lotus's amendment (#1130, 6004659595: never key it on the top level), round 381 (Chat 6004714484, agreed by Code 6004769324). Built on #1130. Provisional until play.

## Context

Round 380 found that a real player almost never reaches Rook's unique door: she joins at the raid's camp at the living median (1 on seed 644's line), and the door asks L7 and lance C. Top level less 2 was proposed and withdrawn after Lotus ruled that a join keyed on the strongest unit rewards feeding one unit. The bare median is the rule already in force and moves nothing.

## Decision

- The `branch` map entry authors the seat: `seatLevel` (the raid: 4) and `seatRank` (30 rank points, rank D). Both are validated on load and need a branch.
- The pick joins at `seatLevel`, or at the living median (`JoinLevel`) when that is higher (`CampaignRecord.SeatLevel`). Nothing reads the company's top level. A test checks that raising the top unit leaves the join unchanged.
- The pick's main weapon is the type of the first weapon on their cast card (Rook: lance, Keziah: axe). Its points are raised to `seatRank` and never lowered (`CampaignRecord.Trained`).
- The passed claimant returns on the field at the pick's level as before. Once the pick has fallen, the return uses the seat's level instead of the bare median, and the returned card carries the same rank floor (6003523643).
- Ordinary joiners, arrivals and meetings keep the bare median. The camp line says `(the seat)` when the seat lifts the pick above the median.
- Both of Rook's doors keep one bar, L7 and rank C.

## Built (#1130), and the calls made in building

- **`campaign --from <map> --pick <id>` (`StartAt`) gives the pick the rank floor but not the seat's level.** Every member of a roster opened this way stands at their cast level, and `--level N` raises them all alike. Seating the pick at 4 first and then raising to N rounded the stats differently, which changed the journaled field plays without any change in play. The field's four journaled replays (1350, 1390, 1400, 1440) replay unchanged.
- **The full-campaign parity script moves from variant 66 to variant 18.** Variant 66 lost the keep once the pick joined at the seat. Variant 18 is the first that wins all ten maps and takes every line `FullCampaignTests` asserts; the Sim reports it missing only `advance` and `refine`. It is regenerated by `--campaign-script 644 --difficulty recruit --permadeath off --variant 18`. CI's godot step names no variant.
- **Gate 1 on the field and #882's price ceiling cannot move.** `--full` plays the standalone map's named roster and `DroverMeasure` is a static combat read, and neither goes through `CampaignRecord`. `--drover` was re-run anyway and is unchanged: player median 0.719, enemy 0.727, passes.
