## Code's notes (the practical side)

**Work however you like.** There is no required workflow for this pass. Draft, restructure, rewrite and throw away whatever serves the story. Creativity needs room, and you have it. There is one hard line: **everything has to be original and ours.** Nothing taken from Fire Emblem or any other existing work, including near-misses: no borrowed characters, lines, scenes or recognisable tropes worn as-is. Lotus will make the final pass, so make it easy for him to see what you changed and why, in whatever form suits the way you worked.

**Where things live:**
- `docs/STORY.md`: the storyline (draft 6, the partners' last version before this pass).
- `docs/voices/`: one voice sheet per character (who they are, how they talk, what they never say).
- `docs/WRITING.md`: the house style and the tone (grounded, a little dry, warm underneath). It's the partners' tool; use what helps.
- `docs/look/CASTING.md`: the portrait and body notes. Lotus will recast after his character pass, so treat it as provisional.
- `docs/DIALOGUE.md`: what the partners have agreed so far, and why.
- `content/campaign.json` and the scene scripts: the text the game actually prints (maps, cards, quests, supports, barks).
- `CLAUDE.md`: how the project and the partners work.

**Keep, because Lotus likes it or decided it:**
- The main storyline's spine: the Kin sealed under the keep in frozen iron. Hask, the captain's father figure, hearing it through his shard. The levy company gathered one fighter at a time. The keep you build up for the final battle. The three endings (the reseal and one nation, the bad ending, Under the Hill).
- **Hask's last line is Lotus's own:** "It said we would be safe. It said the world would be at peace.. but I didn't stop to think what it would cost. Thank you for stopping me.. son." (or "daughter", following the captain's gender). Don't change it.
- **Standing decisions:** Pell is a woman. The drake rider will be renamed by Lotus, and is a woman with silver-white hair and ice-blue eyes. The name Rook moves to the tank character (today's Dunstan).
- Lotus's open idea, recorded at the end of STORY.md: the god in the scythe is **Bane**, the Kin is its **other half**, and the secret ending seals the Kin's half in the scythe too. It isn't confirmed; develop it with him.
- Under discussion, not yet decided (see the Design Table): siding with Hask leading only to the bad ending; magic as basic, fire, water, earth and lightning, plus light (healing) and a dark-magic grimoire that changes Pell, or corrupts Maud and fails her questline.

**Combat, from the story's side.** The story decides **what** each fight is, **where** it happens and **why** it matters. Who is there and what they want, what the ground means, what the player is protecting or chasing, and what winning or losing costs. That shapes the challenge. The exact numbers (how many enemies, how hard they hit, the turn limits) are tuned afterwards through the Sim and the partners' Fun Gate. So write the fight the story needs, say what it should feel like, and let the tuning serve it.

**Two examples of "generic" to calibrate on:**
- **Bet**, the secret barracks hire: "twenty years on a town gate, has opinions about gates" is too close to a well-known Fire Emblem character. She needs a past of her own.
- **Brannock** looks like a hardened fighter in his portrait and is written as a quiet parish cook. It's a mismatch, and it could become a strength or need a rethink. Your call.
