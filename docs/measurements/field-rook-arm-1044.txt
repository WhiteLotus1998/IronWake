Reading: neither the bows' exposure refusal nor the level-1 stats. The veto covers only the captain (and a
protect: unit), so every other recruit approaches by section 8 with no exposure check, and the fastest one
arrives alone. Keziah (move 5) arrives with the party; Rook (move 7) does not. The 76 attacks in 200 games
in field-rook-arm-diagnostic.txt are the games where she survives turn 1 or 2. This is the baseline's
known blindness ("walks into sleeping groups"), and it bites hardest on the fastest unit.
