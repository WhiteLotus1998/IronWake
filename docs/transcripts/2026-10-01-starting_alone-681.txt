-- Starting Alone --
The appointment came with a seal, a coat, and a list. The list is the
company: ten names from three regions, owed to Ironwake under the
armistice. None of them has arrived. I read it every morning anyway. So
far it is the only part of the job I have done.

The keep is two days east. Someone has followed me since the ford, and
the waystation ahead has a fort with its gate still on. A fort keeps the
weather off. It does not keep anyone from shooting at it.

What the crown taught me before it sent me, which was not a lot: read
the blow before you swing it. Know who answers back and who cannot. A
sword answers a sword. It does not answer a bow.

the chaplain lost her two nights ago, the keeper says, and she has been
holding the miller's house on the far bank since. Alone, two days. That
is longer than I have managed anything.

whether she can fight. It says she mends things.
