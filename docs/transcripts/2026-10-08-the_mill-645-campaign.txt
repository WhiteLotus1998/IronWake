-- Starting Alone --
Ironwake, before sunrise. Rime whitens the walls to the hoardings. In
the yard Hask has both hands on the girth of Alder's horse.
Hask: He still blows his belly out for the cinch. You were nine the
first time he had you off on the road.
Hask waits for the horse to breathe out and takes the strap up a hole.
Alder Fenn: None of them has reported, sir.
Hask: They won't come to a keep. They'll come to whoever walks to their
door.
Alder unfolds the list against the saddle. Ten names in a clerk's hand.
The first is struck through, and Alder's thumb stays on it.
Alder Fenn: Who struck the first line?
Hask: The clerk copied the armistice roll, and the roll is eight winters
stale. Ask at every door twice.
He hands up a purse. It is lighter than a purse for ten should be, and
he does not pretend otherwise.
Hask: Bread and a bed for ten until the thaw. Not a week past it.
Hask: Aldmere will swear it sent its men, and Sallow will swear Aldmere
lied. Kestrow won't answer the letter.
Hask: Count them before you draw. The bowman will keep to the high
ground and let you come to him.
Alder Fenn: I'll write from each door.
Hask: Don't write, Alder. Bring them home.
The sergeant: She sticks at the half in a frost, Warden. Give her a
minute.
Hask puts two fingers under the girth. It is tight. He checks it again
anyway, and behind him the gate starts to grind open.
