# Notes for the future

Ideas Lotus wants kept for after this game ships. Nothing here is scheduled or built; it is recorded so it is not lost.

## A second Ironwake

- **Lotus, 2026-10-02:** "The sequel will carry over your save file and depending on the ending you get, the story will continue from there."
- What that asks of this game, at almost no cost now: the save file records the ending reached (the reseal, held or hand-locked; the secret ending, the god killed or not; the bad ending) and the choices that shaped it (the branch pick and the passed claimant's fate, who lived and who fell, Marrit's fate, Kinsbane's state, the captain's origin, gender and class), in a form a later game can read.
- Hooks the story already leaves open (STORY.md): the seal renewed every year in the reseal ending; Kinsbane's old god walking free if the Kin is killed in the secret ending; the husks who never came back.
