b11 hask: Aldmere will swear it sent its men, and Sallow will swear Aldmere lied. Kestrow won't answer the letter.

b13 captain: I'll write from each door.
b14 hask: Don't write, Alder. Bring them home.
b15 sergeant: She sticks at the half in a frost, Warden. Give her a minute.
b16 narration: Hask puts two fingers under the girth. It is tight. He checks it again anyway, and behind him the gate starts to grind open.
